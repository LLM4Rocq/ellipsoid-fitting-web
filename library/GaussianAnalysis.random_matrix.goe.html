For a of law gauss (d * d), goe_mx a is the symmetric matrix W = (A + A^T) / sqrt (2 d) with A = vec_mx a: the entries W_ij, i <= j, are independent centered normal with variance 1 / d off the diagonal and 2 / d on it. This normalization has the semicircle law of radius 2 (semicircle_prob 2 of probability/semicircle_law.v) as limit of its spectrum. The Gaussian features of Koehler and Sohn at kappa = 3 (arXiv 2608.27372, lines 1004-1086) have the same law up to mxvec. This file is part of item G7 of phase 5.

Proved:

  • polyfun F: F is continuous on 'rV[R]_m and of polynomial growth, the hypothesis of Gaussian integration by parts (gauss_ibp); it holds for constants and coordinates and is stable under sums and products (polyfunD, polyfunM, polyfun_sum), hence for the entries of sums, products and powers of matrices with such entries (mx_polyfun) and for their traces.
  • is_derive_mxpow: the derivative of M |-> (M ^+ k) i j at W along E is the entry (i, j) of mxpow_deriv W E k = \sum_(r < k) W^r E W^(k-1-r); mxpow_deriv_trace: for W symmetric, \sum_(i, j) of the entries (j, i) of mxpow_deriv W (E_ij + E_ji) k is k tr W^(k-1) + \sum_(r < k) tr W^r tr W^(k-1-r).
  • gauss_ibp_polyfun: Gaussian integration by parts E[x_p F x] = E[d_p F x] for F and d_p F of polynomial growth.
  • goe_schwinger_dyson: the Schwinger-Dyson equation for the moments of W, E tr W^(k+1) = (k E tr W^(k-1) + \sum_(r < k) E[tr W^r tr W^(k-1-r)]) / d, by integration by parts in each coordinate a_ij of a; with t_k = tr W^k / d it reads E t_(k+1) = (k / d) E t_(k-1) + \sum_(r < k) E[t_r t_(k-1-r)].
  • The operator norm: frobenius W <= sqrt (2 / d) |a| (frobenius_goe_mx), so opnorm W is sqrt (2 / d)-Lipschitz in a (goe_opnorm_lipschitz); E opnorm W <= 2 sqrt 2 for d >= 1 (goe_opnorm_mean, from gauss_opnorm_mean and E |g| <= sqrt n, gauss_frobenius_mean); and E exp (s opnorm W) <= exp (2 sqrt 2 s + s^2 / d) for s >= 0 (goe_opnorm_mgf, by Gaussian concentration).
  • goe_tr_var: with t_k = goe_tr k = tr W^k / d, for every u >= 0, Var t_k <= 2 L^2 + 8 (2k)! exp (2 sqrt 2 (2u + 1) + (2u + 1)^2 / d - 6u) with L = sqrt 2 k 3^(k-1) / d. The statistic goe_clip k of the eigenvalues clipped to [-3, 3] is L-Lipschitz in a (goe_clip_lipschitz, by the Hoffman-Wielandt inequality), so its variance is at most L^2 (gauss_poincare_lipschitz), and |t_k - goe_clip k| <= 2 opnorm W^k exp (u (opnorm W - 3)) (goe_clip_err). With u = sqrt d, Var t_k tends to 0: goe_tr_var_small, Var t_k <= eps for every large d.
  • goe_tr_sd: E t_(k+2) = (k + 1) / d E t_k + \sum_(r <= k) E[t_r t_(k-r)], and Rintegral_cov_le: |Cov (X, Y)| <= (Var X + Var Y) / 2.
  • goe_opnorm_tail_small: for c > 2 sqrt 2 and eps > 0, P(opnorm W >= c) <= eps for every large d, from gauss_tail_expR (Markov's inequality for exp (s F)) and goe_opnorm_mgf.

The convergence of E t_k to the moments of the semicircle law and Wigner's law are in wigner.v (goe_moment_cvg, wigner).
