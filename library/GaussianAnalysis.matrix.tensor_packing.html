Rows x_1, ..., x_m in the unit ball of 'rV[R]_n1 and y_1, ..., y_m in the unit ball of 'rV[R]_n2 are almost orthogonal: |<x_a, x_b>| <= eta and |<y_a, y_b>| <= eta for a != b outside two relations Gx and Gy with at most L successors at every vertex. Then few of the tensors x_a^T y_a of 'M[R]_(n1, n2), with the Frobenius inner product, have a large projection on a subspace of small dimension: if A_1, ..., A_r are Frobenius-orthonormal with r <= d, 0 < eps <= 1, d >= 1 and 0 <= eta <= eps^2 / 10^4, then at most

30000 (1 + L) / eps^2 exp (30000 sqrt d ln (2 + d) / eps^2)

indices a have sum_k (x_a A_k y_a^T)^2 >= eps^2, the left side being the squared norm of the projection of x_a^T y_a on the span of the A_k.

This file is a Rocq formalization of the credited argument of Theorem 4.1, 'Tensor packing', of 'Pointwise convergence of fourfold ergodic averages for mixing transformations' (OpenAI, 4 October 2026), family 154 of the OpenAI math collection at revision 1581d4cbd (sections/04-packing.tex). The same theorem is in the section 'Tensor packing' of its companion 'Pointwise Multiple Ergodic Averages for Mixing Transformations'. There is no Lean version. The proof is the manuscript's:

  • the spectral subspaces E_1 of \sum_k A_k A_k^T and E_2 of \sum_k A_k^T A_k for the eigenvalues larger than sqrt d have dimension at most sqrt d, since both traces are r <= d;
  • few indices a have a projection of x_a on E_1, or of y_a on E_2, of norm larger than eps / 10: cover these projections by balls of radius eps / 40 and apply the cluster bound to each ball;
  • for the other indices, the coefficients v_a of x_a^T y_a on the compressions B_k = Q_1 A_k Q_2, where Q_i projects on the orthogonal complement of E_i, have norm at least 4 eps / 5, so the clusters around a maximal eps / 8-separated set J of them have at most 3 (1 + L) / eps^2 indices each;
  • Sudakov's minoration (comparison/sudakov.v) bounds sqrt (ln #|J|) by a multiple of E opnorm (\sum_k g_k B_k) / eps, and the Gaussian matrix series bound gauss_mx_series_opnorm_log of random_matrix/matrix_series.v bounds this expectation by 5 d^(1/4) sqrt (ln (2 + d)), since \sum_k B_k B_k^T and \sum_k B_k^T B_k have operator norm at most sqrt d and trace at most d.

The formalization differs from the manuscript in four points:

  • the dimensions are finite, and the projection of rank at most d is given by an orthonormal basis A_1, ..., A_r of its range; the reduction from Hilbert spaces to finite spans and the consequence for d_m = (1 + log m)^(3/2) are not formalized;
  • the relations need not be symmetric, and their degree is the number of successors;
  • the clusters of the remaining indices use the relation Gx alone, with |<x_a^T y_a, x_b^T y_b>| = |<x_a, x_b>| |<y_a, y_b>| <= eta, where the manuscript uses the union of the two graphs and eta^2;
  • the constant is explicit, C = 30000, and the coverings are those of net_exists (matrix/nets.v), with (2 e (1 + 80 / eps))^k balls in dimension k where the manuscript has (1 + 80 / eps)^k.

Proved:

  • almost_orthogonal_sum_le, the cluster bound: if <v_a, v_a> <= 1 and |<v_a, v_b>| <= eta for a != b outside a relation with at most L successors, then |\sum_(a in S) v_a|^2 <= (1 + L) |S| + eta |S|^2; almost_orthogonal_card_le: if moreover |\sum_(a in S) v_a| >= rho |S| with rho >= 0 and rho^2 > eta, then |S| <= (1 + L) / (rho^2 - eta).
  • large_projection_card: for such columns X_a in the unit ball and V with orthonormal rows (V V^T = 1) in 'M[R]_(k, n), at most (2 e (1 + 80 / eps))^k 200 (1 + L) / eps^2 indices have |V X_a| > eps / 10, when 0 <= eta <= eps^2 / 10^4.
  • bessel_mxdot, Bessel's inequality: \sum_k <Z, A_k>^2 <= <Z, Z> for a Frobenius-orthonormal family, so the coefficient map mxcoef A is a contraction (frobenius_mxcoef_le).
  • maximal_separated_subset: every finite set I of indices has a delta-separated subset J such that every point of I is at distance less than delta of a point of J.
  • spectral_rowperp_form_le: for P orthogonal and t >= 0, the quadratic form of P^T diag(e) P on the range of rowperp V, where V = select_rows P [set u | t < e_u], is at most t |z|^2.
  • opnorm_compress_gram, trace_compress_gram: if the quadratic form of \sum_k A_k A_k^T on the range of rowperp V1 is at most t |z|^2, with t >= 0, then the compressions B_k = rowperp V1 A_k rowperp V2 satisfy opnorm (\sum_k B_k B_k^T) <= t and tr (\sum_k B_k B_k^T) <= \sum_k |A_k|^2.
  • sudakov_mx_series: if the coefficient vectors on B_1, ..., B_r of the tensors x_i^T y_i of l + 1 pairs of rows in the unit balls are delta-separated, then for g of law gauss r, delta (sqrt (ln (l + 1)) / 2 - 2) <= sqrt 2 E opnorm (\sum_k g_k B_k).
  • tensor_packing: the theorem, with the constant 30000.
