A Rocq formalization of the variance step of the credited argument of the proposition 'Polynomial concentration' of 'A limiting satisfiability threshold for every fixed clause size' (family 235 of the OpenAI math collection, revision 1581d4cbd, source/sections/concentration.tex), in the recursive form of the Lean development of the collection, which replaces the lemmas 'Deletion variance bound' and 'Pivotal pairs' of the manuscript and which this file follows (lean/OAI/Probability/RandomSAT):

S is a set of assignments on n variables, and the clauses of size k are uniform and independent; the means are those of finite_mean.v.

  • clause_avg k f S is the mean of f (add_clause S c) over one clause c, tuple_avg k m f S the mean of f (restrict S t) over m clauses t, survival k m S the probability that S survives m clauses, and kill k S the probability that one clause empties S. survival_le_geom: survival k d S <= (1 - kill k S)^d, since each of the d clauses must spare S.
  • life_mean m S and life_var m S are the mean and the variance of the lifetime life S t over m clauses t.
  • life_var_succ_le: by the law of total variance for the first clause (unif_var_tuple_cons), the variance over m.+1 clauses is at most the mean over the first clause of the variance over the other m, plus the second moment second m S of the change of the lifetime when a clause is inserted in front (delay).
  • With trunc M S = min(M kill k S, 1) and occ f m S the sum over j < m of the mean of f after j clauses, secondS and pivotal_local give second m S <= 2 occ (trunc M) m.+1 S for m < M (second_le_occ).
  • life_var_le_occ: if clause n k is not empty (k <= n), then life_var m S <= 2 m occ (trunc M) m S for m <= M.

The manuscript omits the clause i, bounds Var V by the sum over i of E (V^(-i) - V)^2 (the omission form of the Efron-Stein inequality, efron_stein_omission in library/probability/efron_stein_omission.v, which the proof of T7 does not use), and counts the pairs of pivotal times. The recursion here inserts a clause in front instead, as the Lean development does, and gives a bound of the same form: 2 M times the sum over the M prefixes of the mean of min(M q, 1), with q the killing probability.

life_var_le_occ
For k <= n, a set S of assignments on n variables and m <= M, the variance of the lifetime life S t of m independent uniform clauses t of size k is at most 2 m times the sum over j < m of the mean of min(M q_j, 1), where q_j is the probability that one more clause empties the solution set of the first j clauses.

forall {R : realType} [n k : nat],
(0 < #|{: clause n k}|)%N ->
forall [m M : nat] (S0 : {set {ffun 'I_n -> bool}}),
(m <= M)%N -> life_var k m S0 <= 2 * m%:R * occ k (trunc k M) m S0

The variance of the lifetime is at most 2 m times the occupation sum of the truncated killing chance.

Main result, as the README states it

For k <= n, a set S of assignments on n variables and m <= M, the variance of the lifetime life S t of m independent uniform clauses t of size k is at most 2 m times the sum over j < m of the mean of min(M q_j, 1), where q_j is the probability that one more clause empties the solution set of the first j clauses.
