Exponential moments and tails of the normal law

For s != 0, normal_prob m s is the normal law of mean m and variance s^2 of MathComp-Analysis. This file computes its moment generating function, E[expR (t X)] = expR (t m + t^2 s^2 / 2) for X of law normal_prob m s (integral_normal_expR), and deduces Chernoff bounds for its tails: P(X >= m + u) and P(X <= m - u) are at most expR (- u^2 / (2 s^2)) for u >= 0 (normal_prob_upper_tail, normal_prob_lower_tail), and P(|X - m| >= u) is at most twice that (normal_prob_abs_tail).

measure_ge_expR is Chernoff's bound in integral form: P(u <= f) <= E[expR (t (f - u))] for every measure P, measurable f and t >= 0.
