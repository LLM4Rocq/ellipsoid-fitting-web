The bound of Lemma 3.5 of Koehler and Sohn's vector small-ball estimate: for a law mu on the line with E[X^2] <= v and Q(mu, lambda) <= 1 - eps, and for 0 < r <= 1/2 and r <= s,

\int |cf mu u|^(1/r^2) exp (- s^2 u^2 / (2 r^2)) du <= r C,
C = 16 pi^3 sqrt pi e^(1/8) (1 + sqrt (4 v / eps)) / (lambda sqrt eps)

(gauss_factor_le; gauss_factor_scaled_le after the substitution u = r t; gauss_factor_exists with the constant uniform in mu). The same holds with E[(X - a)^2] <= v for any a, the variance when a is the mean (gauss_factor_center_le, gauss_factor_center_exists). With N = floor (1 / (2 r^2)), |cf mu u|^(1/r^2) <= |cf mu u|^(2 N), the characteristic function of the sum S of N independent copies of the symmetrized law (cf_re_sum_sym). The Gaussian Fubini identity

\int cf_re nu u exp (- u^2 / 2) du = sqrt (2 pi) E[exp (- X^2 / 2)]

(Rintegral_cf_re_gauss_weight), the bound

E[exp (- X^2 / 2)] <= 2 e^(1/8) sqrt (2 pi) Q(nu, 1)

for X of law nu (gauss_weight_conc_fun), and the concentration of sums of esseen.v, Q(S, 1) <= C' / sqrt N <= 2 C' r, conclude.

The first half of the same lemma, for any truncation level c: a law with mean 0, variance 1 and E[X^2 1_{|X| <= M}] >= c has, for every L > 0,

Q(mu, L) <= 1 - min ((c - 4 L^2) / M^2, L^2 / (2 L^2 + 1))

(conc_fun_truncated_level, conc_fun_truncated_level_exists). For c = 1/2 and L = 1/8 this gives the paper's Q(mu, 1/8) <= 1 - min (7 / (16 M^2), 1/100) (conc_fun_truncated, conc_fun_truncated_exists). The length must depend on c: if c < 1/256, a law with mass close to 1 at +-1/16 and a small mass far away has Q(mu, 1/8) close to 1.
