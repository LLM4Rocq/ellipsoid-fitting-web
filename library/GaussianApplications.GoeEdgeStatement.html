The statement of a Rocq formalization of the credited argument of 'Cutoff throughout the high-temperature Sherrington-Kirkpatrick phase' (family 227 of the OpenAI math collection, revision 1581d4cbd): the lemma 'Gaussian concentration and comparison' and the first part of the proof of the proposition 'Good disorder' (appendices/entropy.tex). This file holds the statement only. The Lean counterparts, in lean/OAI/Probability/SKRatio of the collection:

Lean: goe_expected_top (Matrices/DiagonalTMemLp.lean, line 93)

Lean: goe_norm_tail (Matrices/GoeMatrixQuadCoeff.lean, line 115)

Setting. R is any realType and d >= 1. a has law gauss (d * d), the standard Gaussian measure on 'rV[R]_(d * d) (gaussian/gauss.v), and W = goe_mx a is the GOE matrix (A + A^T) / sqrt (2 d) of random_matrix/goe.v, A = vec_mx a: its entries W_ij, i <= j, are independent centered normal, of variance 1 / d off the diagonal and 2 / d on it. lambda_max is the supremum of x^T W x over the unit vectors x (matrix/lambda_max.v), opnorm the operator norm (matrix/opnorm.v). Probabilities are values of gauss (d * d), extended reals; E is the integral against it.

goe_edge_statement, for every R and every d >= 1:

(a) a |-> lambda_max (goe_mx a) is measurable and integrable against gauss (d * d);

(b) E lambda_max W <= 2;

(c) for every t >= 0, P(lambda_max W >= 2 + t) <= exp (- d t^2 / 4), and P(lambda_max (- W) >= 2 + t) <= exp (- d t^2 / 4);

(d) for every t >= 0, P(opnorm W >= 2 + t) <= 2 exp (- d t^2 / 4).

Choices, for the reviewer.

  • (b) bounds the expectation of lambda_max W, not of opnorm W, as the candidates review asks; opnorm W = max (lambda_max W, lambda_max (- W)) for symmetric W (opnorm_lambda_max), which gives (d) from (c).
  • The tail constant d / 4 is the sharp one of Gaussian concentration for the sqrt (2 / d)-Lipschitz function a |-> lambda_max (goe_mx a). The TeX states its concentration lemma with the constant pi^2 / 8 of the Maurey-Pisier argument, and the second Lean theorem cited above bounds the tail of the norm by 2 exp (- eps^2 d / (pi^2 j)) at the scale j of its matrices. The statement here is stronger than both.
  • The manuscript writes the bound for the matrix beta W of its coupling; the statement is at beta = 1, the library normalization of goe_mx, and the scaling to beta W is linear.

Definition goe_edge_statement : Prop :=
  forall (R : realType) (d : nat), (0 < d)%N ->
  [/\ measurable_fun [set: 'rV[R]_(d * d)] (fun a : 'rV[R]_(d * d) => lambda_max (goe_mx a)) /\
      (gauss (d * d)).-integrable [set: 'rV[R]_(d * d)]
        (EFin \o (fun a : 'rV[R]_(d * d) => lambda_max (goe_mx a))),
      \int[gauss (d * d)]_a lambda_max (goe_mx a) <= (2 : R),
      forall t : R, 0 <= t ->
        (gauss (d * d) [set a | (2 + t <= lambda_max (goe_mx a))%R] <=
           (expR (- (d%:R * t ^+ 2 / 4)))%:E)%E /\
        (gauss (d * d) [set a | (2 + t <= lambda_max (- goe_mx a))%R] <=
           (expR (- (d%:R * t ^+ 2 / 4)))%:E)%E &
      forall t : R, 0 <= t ->
        (gauss (d * d) [set a | (2 + t <= opnorm (goe_mx a))%R] <=
           (2 * expR (- (d%:R * t ^+ 2 / 4)))%:E)%E].
