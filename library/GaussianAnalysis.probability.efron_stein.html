For x of law tuple_prod mu n (lindeberg.v), x^(i) is x with its coordinate i replaced by an independent t of law mu i: tuple_set x i t. Variances are those of probability.v, with values in [0, +oo]: 'V_P[X] is +oo when X is measurable and not square integrable (varianceT, sqr_dev_variance).

  • Fubini at one coordinate, for F measurable and nonnegative (ge0_integral_tuple_prod_set) and for F integrable (integral_tuple_prod_set): \int[tuple_prod mu n]_x F x is \int[tuple_prod mu n]_x \int[mu i]_t F (tuple_set x i t). The inner integral is a measurable function of x (measurable_tuple_set_integral, measurable_integral_tuple_set), and for F integrable, t |-> F (tuple_set x i t) is integrable for almost every x (ae_integrable_tuple_set).
  • sqr_dev_variance: E (X - c)^2 = Var X + (E X - c)^2 for X measurable and c real; variance_le_sqr_dev: Var X <= E (X - c)^2; variance_resample: E (X - X')^2 = 2 Var X for an independent copy X'.
  • efron_stein_pair: for F integrable on a product of two probabilities, Var F <= E_y Var_x F (x, y) + E_x Var_y F (x, y).
  • efron_stein_integrable (F integrable) and efron_stein (F in L^2): 'V_(tuple_prod mu n)[F] <= \sum_(i < n) \int[tuple_prod mu n]_x V i x, where V i x = 'V_(mu i)[fun t => F (tuple_set x i t)].
  • efron_stein_resample: Var F <= 1/2 \sum_i E (F (x) - F (x^(i)))^2, since E (F (x) - F (x^(i)))^2 = 2 E V i (integral_resample_tuple_set).
  • One coordinate and the others: the coordinate i of tuple_prod mu n has law mu i (ge0_integral_tuple_prod_coord, integral_tuple_prod_coord), and E [f (x_i) H (x)] = E [f] E [H] when H does not depend on x_i (ge0_integral_tuple_prod_coordM, integral_tuple_prod_coordM, with the integrability of the product).
  • cauchy_schwarz: (int |f g|)^2 <= int f^2 int g^2, from Hoelder.
  • Moments from Efron-Stein (the L^p recursion of Koehler and Sohn, in the proof of their Proposition 2.6, at p = 4): efron_stein_fourth, if E (F (x) - F (x^(i)))^4 <= D_i^2 for every i, then E (F - E F)^4 <= 9/2 (sum_i D_i)^2; by Efron-Stein for (F - E F)^2, Cauchy-Schwarz and the two estimates resample_sqr_le and resample_sqr_sqr_le of one resampled coordinate.
