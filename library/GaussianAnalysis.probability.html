probability/

GaussianAnalysis.probability24 files14,080 lines557 lemmas and theorems, 76 definitions

Paley-Zygmund and other moment inequalities, the Lindeberg replacement method, subgaussian and subexponential variables (Hoeffding, Bernstein, Hanson-Wright), the norms of random vectors and matrices with subgaussian coordinates, the Efron-Stein inequality, product laws by rows and conditioning on coordinates, measurable choices of minimizers, symmetrization and the small-ball method, the semicircle law and the scalar variational problem of the Gaussian model, and its version over clipped profiles with its dual form, the omission form of the Efron-Stein inequality.

Guide

Written for this documentation from the sources and the README, and checked against the Rocq statements. The file headers and the statements are the reference.

This directory holds the library's general probability tools, mostly built on tuple_prod, the law of n independent random elements with laws mu 0, ..., mu (n - 1). Independent variables are functions f_i (x_i) of the coordinates of a tuple x of this law.

Some files prove classical nonasymptotic inequalities, with explicit constants: Paley-Zygmund, Efron-Stein, Hoeffding, Bernstein, Hanson-Wright, symmetrization and the small-ball method. Others treat mixtures, total variation, densities, the semicircle law, the scalar variational problem of Koehler and Sohn and, in general form, steps of the manuscripts of applications/.

  2. tuple_rows.v: Fubini, independent rows and blocks, coordinatewise images.
  3. moments.v: the Paley-Zygmund inequality.

  9. hanson_wright.v: the Hanson-Wright inequality.

Core definitions

  • tuple_prod (lindeberg.v): tuple_prod mu n, the product of mu 0, ..., mu (n - 1) on n-tuples, by recursion on n.
  • tuple_set (lindeberg.v): x with coordinate i replaced by t.
  • tuple_merge (conditioning.v): the coordinates of x in S and of y outside S. An event E depends only on S when E (tuple_merge S x y) = E x for all x, y.
  • subgaussian, subexponential (subgaussian.v): X measurable, K > 0 and E exp(X^2 / K^2) <= 2, respectively L > 0 and E exp(|X| / L) <= 2.
  • mixture, cmixture (mixture.v, countable_mixture.v): A |-> sum_i w_i P_i(A), for weights w_i >= 0 of sum one.
  • density_law (density_law.v): A |-> int_A f dmu, for f measurable, nonnegative and of integral one (prob_density).
  • rademacher_mean (symmetrization.v): the average over the 2^n sign vectors.
  • upper_moment, upper_cutoff, upper_value (variational.v): m(w) = E max(X - w, 0) and s(w) = E max(X - w, 0)^2, the cutoff sup {v | v + z <= c m(v)}, and s + c m^2 there.

Main results

Product laws.

  • lindeberg_replacement: for F bounded and measurable on n-tuples, if replacing mu i by nu i at coordinate i moves the integral of F by at most B i whatever the other coordinates, the integrals of F under the two product laws differ by at most sum_i B i. tuple_prod_tv applies it to indicators of measurable sets, with B i = tv_dist (mu i) (nu i).
  • efron_stein: Var F <= sum_i E Var_i F for F square integrable, Var_i being the variance in coordinate i, the others fixed. efron_stein_omission: Var F <= sum_i E (G_i - F)^2 for F integrable and measurable G_i not depending on coordinate i.

Concentration (X_i = f_i (x_i), centered).

  • subgaussian_sum (Hoeffding): if X_i is subgaussian with constant K_i and S = sum_i a_i^2 K_i^2 > 0, then sum_i a_i X_i is subgaussian with constant 4 sqrt S. bernstein (subgaussian.v): if X_i is subexponential with constant L_i, then P(|sum_i a_i X_i| >= t) <= 2 exp(-min(t^2 / (64 sum_i a_i^2 L_i^2), t / (4 max_i |a_i| L_i))) for t >= 0.
  • hanson_wright: if every X_i is subgaussian with constant K, then for an n x n matrix A, Q = sum_(i,j) a_ij X_i X_j has P(|Q - sum_i a_ii E X_i^2| >= u) <= 2 exp(-min(u^2 / (6912 K^4 |A|_F^2), u / (136 K^2 opnorm A))) for u >= 0. subgaussian_opnorm_tail: for an m x n matrix of such entries and t >= 0, P(opnorm > 21 K (sqrt m + sqrt n + t)) <= 2 exp(-t^2).

Empirical processes.

  • small_ball_method: let n, u, q > 0 and measurable f_j with mu_k(|f_j| >= 2 u) >= q for all j and k < n. If the expected Rademacher average of max_(j < J) |(1/n) sum_k eps_k f_j(x_k)| is at most r for every J, the event that some f_j has sum_k f_j(x_k)^2 < n u^2 q / 2 has probability at most 8 r / (u q).

Mixtures and variational problems.

  • tuple_prod_mixture, tuple_prod_cmixture: n independent draws from a finite or countable mixture are the mixture, over label tuples s, of the products of the P (s_k), with weights prod_k w (s_k).
  • upper_variational: for a law on the line with E X^2 < +oo and c > -1, the supremum of max(E (X + z) f(X), 0)^2 / (E f(X)^2 + c (E f(X))^2) over measurable f >= 0 with f^2 integrable and E f > 0 is s(w) + c m(w)^2, where w is the unique root of w + z = c m(w) (upper_cutoff_unique).

Tools of applications/.

  • wpoincare_from_square: let w be positive weights of sum one, and L linear, self-adjoint and nonnegative in L^2(w), with the constants as kernel. If k (f, L f)_w <= (L f, L f)_w for every f, then k Var_w(f) <= (f, L f)_w.
  • rademacher_gauss_lindeberg: for F bounded and measurable on row vectors of length n, with third derivative along coordinate j at most c_j in absolute value, the average of F over the 2^n sign vectors and its integral under gauss n differ by at most (1/2) sum_j c_j.

How the proofs go

  • hanson_wright decouples the off-diagonal part over sets of coordinates (offdiag_decouple) and applies Hoeffding twice with a Gaussian chaos bound; the diagonal uses Bernstein.
  • small_ball_method uses symmetrization, contraction and Markov's inequality, without a concentration inequality. rademacher_gauss_lindeberg replaces one coordinate at a time, with Taylor's formula of order two.

Connections

Outside the directory, some files require gaussian/, comparison/, matrix/ and, for measurable_minimizer.v, convex/. It is used by comparison/, matrix/, convex/, fourier/ (only lindeberg.v), random_matrix/ (wigner.v, restricted_isometry.v) and applications/: T4 uses finite_gap.v, T6 log_partition.v and rademacher_lindeberg.v, and T7 finite_mean.v. The extension/ proofs use it widely, and theories/ uses moments.v.
