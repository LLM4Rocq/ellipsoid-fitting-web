Part of target T7 of phase 12, the capped variance bound for random k-SAT of SatVarianceStatement.v. A Rocq formalization of the credited argument of the proposition 'Polynomial concentration' of 'A limiting satisfiability threshold for every fixed clause size' (family 235 of the OpenAI math collection, revision 1581d4cbd, source/sections/concentration.tex and forcing.tex). The files of RandomSAT follow the route of the Lean development of the collection (lean/OAI/Probability/RandomSAT, paths below relative to it): the variance by a recursion over the clauses, and the forced variables by the erasure of one variable. The clauses, the cap and the statistic V_n are those of SatVarianceDefinitions.v. The assignments on n variables are {ffun 'I_n -> bool}, and S is a set of assignments.

  • add_clause S c is the set of the elements of S that satisfy the clause c, restrict S s the set of those that satisfy every clause of the sequence s, and alive S is 1 if S is not empty and 0 otherwise.
  • life S s, the lifetime, is the number of nonempty sets among the solution sets of the nonempty prefixes of s (life_sum).
  • sat_prefix_lenE: for every tuple t of M clauses, sat_prefix_len t is life [set: _] t. The statistic of the statement, the largest satisfiable prefix length up to the cap, is the lifetime of the set of all assignments. The Lean development states its variance bound for the lifetime; this identity transfers it to the statistic of the statement.
  • The first variable of a clause: a clause of size k.+1 on n.+1 variables either does not contain the first variable, and is cabsent c for a clause c of size k.+1 on the other n variables, or contains it with a sign b, and is cpresent c b for a clause c of size k. The map cjoin of the two cases is a bijection (cjoin_bij). Hence #|clause n k| = 'C(n, k) 2^k (card_clause), and the uniform mean over clause n.+1 k.+1 is the mixture of the two cases with weights 1 - k.+1/n.+1 and k.+1/n.+1 (unif_mean_clause_split).

For a tuple t of M clauses, the largest satisfiable prefix length sat_prefix_len t, the cap M included, is the lifetime life [set: _] t: the number of nonempty solution sets among those of the nonempty prefixes of t. #|clause n k| = 'C(n, k) 2^k. The uniform clause on n.+1 variables: its first variable is absent with probability 1 - k.+1 / n.+1, and the rest is a uniform clause of size k.+1; or present with probability k.+1 / n.+1, with a uniform sign, and the rest is a uniform clause of size k.
