Capped variance of random k-SAT: the definitions

Target T7 of phase 12 (AGENTS.md, section Phase 12; the plan notes/phase12-plan.md, section 3). The definitions of the statement of SatVarianceStatement.v, from 'A limiting satisfiability threshold for every fixed clause size' (family 235 of the OpenAI math collection, revision 1581d4cbd, source/sections/introduction.tex and concentration.tex). The Lean counterparts, in lean/OAI/Probability/RandomSAT of the collection:

Lean: ProperClause, SatisfiesClause (Model.lean)

Lean: capMultiplier, mainCap, variance_capped (Concentration.lean)

All are finite; R is any realType.

  • A proper signed k-clause on n variables is a partial sign assignment c of {ffun 'I_n -> option bool} with exactly k signed variables (proper_clause): c i = Some true is the literal x_i, c i = Some false the literal not x_i, and c i = None means that x_i does not occur. The clauses of size k form the finite type clause n k; there are 2^k binom(n, k) of them, as in the manuscript, and the uniform law on clause n k gives each the same probability.
  • An assignment sigma of {ffun 'I_n -> bool} satisfies c when some literal of c is true (clause_sat). For a tuple t of M clauses, prefix_sat t m says that some assignment satisfies the clauses of t at the positions i < m; prefix_sat t 0 holds.
  • sat_cap k n = 2^(k+1) n is the cap L_k n of the manuscript, and sat_prefix_len t is the largest m <= M with prefix_sat t m: the length of the longest satisfiable prefix of t, capped at M. For M = sat_cap k n it is the statistic V_n = min(H_n - 1, L_k n) of the manuscript, written directly on the first L_k n clauses, so that no clause beyond the cap is drawn.
  • sat_mean F is the average of F over all tuples of M clauses, its expectation when the M clauses are independent and uniform on clause n k (drawn with replacement), and sat_var F its variance. sat_capped_var n k is the variance of V_n.
