Part of target T4 of phase 12 (AGENTS.md, section Phase 12), the spectral gap of the heat-bath dynamics of the Sherrington-Kirkpatrick model for beta < 1/2. A Rocq formalization of the credited argument of 'Cutoff throughout the high-temperature Sherrington-Kirkpatrick phase' (family 227 of the OpenAI math collection, revision 1581d4cbd, appendices/entropy.tex, subsection 'A two-spin estimate and a uniform gap'). The objects are those of the frozen applications/SKGapDefinitions.v. The file follows the manuscript. In the Lean modules of the collection, the pair law is the product of the two names of the first line below, and v_i is the name of the second line:

Lean: pairBaseMass, pairDensity (SKRatio/Calculus/TwoSpin.lean)

Lean: conditionalVariance (SKRatio/Calculus/PlusSpin.lean)

  • Spin flips: spin_set and its algebra, the involution spin_flip, sum_pair2 (a sum over the cube is half the sum over the pairs x^(i,+), x^(i,-)), and cube_const (a function invariant under every spin_set is constant).
  • sk_tanh and sk_cosh, with expR_spin: exp (b u) = cosh u (1 + b tanh u) for a spin b, and, by monotonicity (MathComp-Analysis ger0_derive1_ndecry), |tanh u| <= |u| and |tanh u - u| <= |u|^3 (sk_tanh_norm_le, sk_tanh_sub_le).
  • For a matrix A and a field theta: the weights are positive and the masses sum to one; ising_mean is linear and monotone; the conditional expectation ising_cexp i given the spins outside i and the projection ising_dproj i = I - E_i satisfy D_i f = (x_i - m_i) d_i f (ising_dproj_eq), nu[(D_i f)^2] = nu[v_i (d_i f)^2] (ising_energy) and nu[g D_i f] = nu[D_i g D_i f] (ising_dproj_adj). The Dirichlet form is the sum of the energies, and it vanishes only on constants (ising_dirichlet_eq0). ising_gen f = sum_i D_i f is minus the generator of the heat-bath dynamics with rate one at every site.
  • ising_weight_block: for A symmetric with zero diagonal and i != j, the weights of the four configurations of the spins i and j with the other spins fixed are K (1 + a x)(1 + b y)(1 + t x y), with K > 0, |a|, |b| < 1 and t = tanh A_ij: the pair law of the manuscript.

For the Ising law nu of a matrix A and a field theta on {-1, 1}^n (the definitions of T4), the conditional expectation E_i given the spins outside i and D_i = I - E_i satisfy D_i f = (x_i - m_i) d_i f, nu[(D_i f)^2] = nu[v_i (d_i f)^2] and nu[g D_i f] = nu[D_i g D_i f]; the Dirichlet form vanishes only on the constants; ising_gen f = sum_i D_i f. For A symmetric with zero diagonal and i != j, the weights of the four configurations of the spins i and j, the other spins fixed, are K (1 + a x)(1 + b y)(1 + t x y) with K > 0, |a|, |b| < 1 and t = tanh A_ij. |tanh u| <= |u| and |tanh u - u| <= |u|^3.

Main result, as the README states it

For the Ising law nu of a matrix A and a field theta on {-1, 1}^n (the definitions of T4), the conditional expectation E_i given the spins outside i and D_i = I - E_i satisfy D_i f = (x_i - m_i) d_i f, nu[(D_i f)^2] = nu[v_i (d_i f)^2] and nu[g D_i f] = nu[D_i g D_i f]; the Dirichlet form vanishes only on the constants; ising_gen f = sum_i D_i f. For A symmetric with zero diagonal and i != j, the weights of the four configurations of the spins i and j, the other spins fixed, are K (1 + a x)(1 + b y)(1 + t x y) with K > 0, |a|, |b| < 1 and t = tanh A_ij. |tanh u| <= |u| and |tanh u - u| <= |u|^3.
