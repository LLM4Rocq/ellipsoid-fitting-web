The objects of the Lindeberg argument of Koehler and Sohn (Universality and sharp thresholds for ellipsoid fitting, arXiv 2608.27372, section 2.2): n features Z_1, ..., Z_n in 'rV[R]_N, the ridge-regularized least squares objective in (theta, b), its infimum over theta in a convex set D with an optional barrier B, and the regular features of their Definition 2.5. Features are n-tuples, so that their law is tuple_prod of probability/lindeberg.v; <x, y> is mxdot x y and |x|^2 is mxdot x x.

ls_objective Z lambda beta theta bF_Z(theta, b; beta), the average (2 n)^-1 sum_i (b - <Z_i, theta>)^2 plus the ridge term beta (lambda |theta|^2 + b^2)
ls_loss Z P theta bsum_(i in P) (b - <Z_i, theta>)^2
ls_ridge Z P s lambda beta theta bs ls_loss Z P theta b + beta (lambda |theta|^2 + b^2)
ls_barrier_objective Z P s lambda beta gamma B theta bls_ridge Z P s lambda beta theta b + gamma B theta; with P the indices other than i and s = (2 n)^-1, the leave-one-out objective F^(i) of the paper
ls_barrier_value Z lambda beta D gamma BPhi_Z(D, B; beta, gamma), the infimum of F_Z(theta, b; beta) + gamma B theta over theta in D and b real
ls_value Z lambda beta DPhi_Z(D; beta), the infimum of F_Z(theta, b; beta) over theta in D and b real; Phi_Z(D) is ls_value Z lambda 0 D, for any lambda
regular_feature K tau Sigma muthe law mu on 'rV[R]_N is a (K, tau)-regular feature with covariance Sigma: lambda = opnorm Sigma > 0, (F1) E <Z, theta>^4 <= K^4 lambda^2 |theta|^4, mean 0, E <Z, theta> <Z, theta'> = theta Sigma theta'^T and (F2) Var (Z T Z^T) <= tau^2 |T|_F^2 for T symmetric

The paper's lambda is the operator norm opnorm Sigma; the definitions take it as a parameter, so that they do not depend on a law.

Main results, for lambda, beta, gamma >= 0 and B >= 0 on D:

  • ls_objective_ge0: F_Z(theta, b; beta) >= 0.
  • ls_barrier_value_le, ls_barrier_value_ge0: Phi_Z(D, B; beta, gamma) is at most F_Z(theta, b; beta) + gamma B theta for every theta in D and every real b, and it is nonnegative.
  • ls_value_barrierE: Phi_Z(D; beta) is the value with a barrier of weight 0; ls_value_le and ls_value_ge0 are the same bounds for it.
  • ls_barrier_value_mono, ls_value_mono: the values are nondecreasing in beta.

Over an empty D both values are 0 (ls_barrier_value_empty).

Elsewhere in the library. The values are measurable functions of the n-tuple Z when D is nonempty, convex and relatively open and B >= 0 is continuous on D (measurable_ls_barrier_value in barrier_lindeberg.v, measurable_ls_value in ridge_comparison.v), and so is the minimizer (measurable_ls_min, probability/measurable_minimizer.v). For a regular feature, the fourth moments of the linear forms are finite and every measurable g with |g z| <= a + b |z|^4 is integrable (reg_int4, reg_integrable in feature_moments.v); under the hypotheses of Theorem 2.9, Phi_Z(D, B; beta, gamma) is integrable for n independent regular features (int_Phi, barrier_lindeberg.v). Gaussian features are regular (gauss_regular_feature, gauss_feature.v).

Definition ls_objective n (Z : n.-tuple 'rV[R]_N) (lambda beta : R)
    (theta : 'rV[R]_N) (b : R) : R :=
  (n%:R *+ 2)^-1 * \sum_(i < n) (b - mxdot (tnth Z i) theta) ^+ 2 +

Definition
Definition regular_feature (K tau : R) (Sigma : 'M[R]_N)
    (mu : probability 'rV[R]_N R) : Prop :=
  [/\ (0 < opnorm Sigma)%R,
      forall theta : 'rV[R]_N,
        'E_mu[fun z => (mxdot z theta ^+ 4)%R] <=
          (K ^+ 4 * opnorm Sigma ^+ 2 * mxdot theta theta ^+ 2)%:E,
      forall theta : 'rV[R]_N, 'E_mu[fun z => mxdot z theta] = 0,
      forall theta theta' : 'rV[R]_N,
        'E_mu[fun z => (mxdot z theta * mxdot z theta')%R] =
          (mxdot (theta *m Sigma) theta')%:E &
      forall T : 'M[R]_N, T \is symmetricmx ->
        'V_mu[fun z => mxdot z (z *m T)] <= (tau ^+ 2 * frobenius T ^+ 2)%:E].
