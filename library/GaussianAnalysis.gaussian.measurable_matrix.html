A matrix with entries in a measurable space is measurable for the sigma-algebra generated by its entries, as tuples are in MathComp-Analysis (measurable_structure.v). Row vectors 'rV[T]_n and column vectors 'cV[T]_n are matrices, so they get this structure too. The boxes generate it and are closed under intersection, so two finite measures that agree on the boxes agree (measure_mxbox_unique). For real matrices it contains the open sets, so continuous functions are measurable (continuous_measurable_mx).

The last sections concern the norm `|A| of a real matrix, which in MathComp is the largest absolute value of an entry: bounds for entries, permuted columns, blocks and products (mx_norm_entry_le, ...), and the continuity of maps into matrices (continuous_mxP) and of matrix products (continuous_mulmxr, continuous_mulmxl).

measure_matrix_display ddisplay of the sigma-algebra of the matrices with entries in a space of display d 'M[T]_(m, n) is equipped with a measurableType structure generated by the entries
mxbox Athe box of the matrices M such that M i j is in A i j for all i and j; for a row vector x, the box [set x | forall j, A j (x 0 j)] is used directly (rowboxE)
mxboxes Tthe boxes mxbox A with measurable sides A i j
