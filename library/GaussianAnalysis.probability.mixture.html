The mixture of probabilities P i, i in a finite type I, with weights w i: the law of a variable drawn from P i with probability w i. The weights are nonnegative of sum one, a proof argument of the mixture, as uniform_prob takes a < b.

mixture_weights ww i >= 0 for every i and \sum_i w i = 1
mixture P hwfor hw : mixture_weights w, the probability A |-> \sum_i w i P i (A)

Main results:

  • mixtureE: mixture P hw A = \sum_i w i P i (A);
  • ge0_integral_mixture, integrable_mixture, integral_mixture, expectation_mixture: \int f d(mixture P hw) = \sum_i w i \int f dP_i for f nonnegative and measurable, or integrable under every P i, and then f is integrable under the mixture; Lfun1_mixture and Lfun2_mixture for L^1 and L^2;
  • variance_mixture: the law of total variance,
    Var X = \sum_i w i (Var_i X + (E_i X - E X)^2);
  • subgaussian_mixture, subexponential_mixture: a variable subgaussian (subexponential) with constant K under every P i is so under the mixture;
  • distribution_mixture: the image of a mixture by a measurable map is the mixture of the images;
  • big_tuple_distr, mixture_weights_prod: \sum over s : n.-tuple I of \prod_k F k (s_k) is \prod_k \sum_i F k i, so the product weights are mixture weights;
  • tuple_prod_mixture: the product of n copies of a mixture is the mixture, over the label tuples s with the product weights, of the products tuple_prod (fun k => P (s_k)) n: on boxes by bigA_distr_bigA, then measure_tuple_box_unique;
  • mixture_cover: measurable sets E i with P i (E i) <= eps that all contain a set F have a measurable intersection, containing F, of mixture probability at most eps.

forall [d : measure_display] [T : measurableType d] [R : realType] 
  [I : finType] [w : I -> R] (P : I -> probability T R) (hw : mixture_weights w) 
  (i0 : I) [n : nat] [A : set (n.-tuple T)],
(measure_tuple_display d).-measurable A ->
tuple_prod (fun=> mixture P hw) n A =
\sum_s ((\prod_(k < n) w (tnth s k))%:E * tuple_prod (fun k : nat => P (nth i0 s k)) n A)%E

The product of n copies of a mixture is the mixture, over the label tuples s with weights \prod_k w (s_k), of the products of the P (s_k); i0 is any label, for the indices past n.
