The spectral gap of the heat-bath dynamics from a bound on its square

Part of target T4 of phase 12 (AGENTS.md, section Phase 12), the spectral gap of the heat-bath dynamics of the Sherrington-Kirkpatrick model for beta < 1/2: the spectral step of item T4a. A Rocq formalization of the credited argument of the end of the proof of the proposition 'Gap and covariance under scaled interactions and fields' (rcut:prop:uniform-gap) of 'Cutoff throughout the high-temperature Sherrington-Kirkpatrick phase' (family 227 of the OpenAI math collection, revision 1581d4cbd, file appendices/entropy.tex). It follows the TeX, not a Lean module of the collection.

Take any matrix A and field theta, the Ising law nu of SKGapDefinitions.v, the projections D_i = I - E_i of SKGap/Ising.v and L = ising_gen A theta = sum_i D_i, the heat-bath generator with rate one at every site, up to sign. In L^2(nu), L is linear (ising_gen_lin) and self-adjoint (ising_gen_sym, from ising_dproj_adj), <f, L f>_nu is the Dirichlet form (ising_gen_form), hence nonnegative, and the kernel of L is the constants (ising_gen_ker, from ising_dirichlet_eq0). So wpoincare_from_square of probability/finite_gap.v applies: if k E(g) <= nu[(L g)^2] for every g, then k Var_nu(f) <= E(f) for every f (ising_gap_from_square). The manuscript applies the bound to a nonconstant eigenfunction of L; the library uses the spectral theorem for the matrix of L.
