For G : 'rV[R]_n -> R and eps > 0, gauss_smooth eps G x is E[G (x + eps y)] for y of law gauss n. If G is bounded by K and L-Lipschitz for the Euclidean norm, then gauss_smooth eps G is bounded by K (gauss_smooth_bounded) and L-Lipschitz (gauss_smooth_lipschitz). Its partial derivatives are gauss_smooth_partial eps G j x = E[G (x + eps y) y_j] / eps (gauss_smooth_derive), computed with the Cameron-Martin formula of gauss_shift.v and differentiation under the integral sign. They are continuous (continuous_gauss_smooth_partial), so gauss_smooth eps G is differentiable (gauss_smooth_differentiable), and its gradient has Euclidean norm at most L (gauss_smooth_grad). Finally |gauss_smooth eps G x - G x| <= L eps E[|y|] (gauss_smooth_close).

The file also proves that a function that is Lipschitz for the Euclidean norm is continuous (lipschitz_frobenius_continuous), that the gradient of a function of class C^1 that is L-Lipschitz has Euclidean norm at most L (lipschitz_frobenius_grad), and an is_derive form of differentiation under the integral sign (is_derive_Rintegral).
