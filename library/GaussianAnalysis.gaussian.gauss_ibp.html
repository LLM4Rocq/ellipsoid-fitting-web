Stein's identity for the standard normal law, E[X f(X)] = E[f'(X)] (normal_ibp), and its version in several dimensions for gauss n, E[x_i F(x)] = E[d_i F(x)] (gauss_ibp), for functions of class C^1 of polynomial growth. gauss_ibp_line asks less: F and d_i F measurable, and d_i F continuous along the lines parallel to the i-th axis. A partial derivative of a measurable function is measurable (measurable_derive_rV), so the identity of order two, E[(x_i^2 - 1) F(x)] = E[d_i d_i F(x)] (gauss_ibp2), holds for F measurable and d_i d_i F continuous, without any continuity of d_i F.

Polynomial growth means `|F x| <= C * (1 + `|x| ^+ k) for all x, where `|x| is the largest absolute value of a coordinate of x (gauss.v, section poly_bound).
