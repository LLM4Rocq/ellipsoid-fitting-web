Ball's geometric form of the Brascamp-Lieb inequality, for the standard Gaussian measure and bounded functions. Let u_1, ..., u_n be unit vectors of 'rV[R]_m and c_1, ..., c_n positive weights such that sum_i c_i u_i^T u_i is the identity matrix of size m: the vectors sqrt (c_i) u_i form a tight frame. For measurable f_1, ..., f_n with values in [delta, K], 0 < delta (gauss_brascamp_lieb_bounded),

int prod_i f_i(<x, u_i>)^{c_i} dgauss_m(x)
  <= prod_i (int f_i d(normal_prob 0 1))^{c_i},

with real integrals and powR. The extension to f_i with values in [0, K] (gauss_brascamp_lieb_ge0) and to measurable F_i : R -> [0, +oo] (gauss_brascamp_lieb), and the form with Lebesgue measures (brascamp_lieb), are derived from this theorem in brascamp_lieb_lebesgue.v.

The proof uses the Mehler transform of ornstein_uhlenbeck.v. For 0 <= r <= 1 let

Lambda(r) = int G_r(x) dgauss_m(x),
G_r(x) = exp (sum_i c_i ln (mehler r f_i)(<x, u_i>)).

Since mehler 1 f = f and mehler 0 f is the mean of f, Lambda(1) is the left side and Lambda(0) the right side. For 0 < r < 1, Lambda is differentiable under the integral. The generator identity r d/dr mehler r f = x (mehler r f)' - (mehler r f)'' and Gaussian integration by parts along each u_i (gauss_ibp_unit) give

r Lambda'(r) = int (|V(x)|^2 - sum_i c_i a_i(x)^2) G_r(x) dgauss_m(x),

where a_i(x) is the derivative of ln (mehler r f_i) at <x, u_i> and V(x) = sum_i c_i a_i(x) u_i. This is nonpositive by a weighted Cauchy-Schwarz inequality for tight frames (tight_frame_cauchy_schwarz). Lambda is continuous at 1 from the left, since mehler r f_i tends to f_i in L^1 of the standard normal law (mehler_cvg_L1), and the mean value theorem gives Lambda(1) <= Lambda(0).

gauss_brascamp_lieb_bounded
The Gaussian Brascamp-Lieb inequality for bounded functions: for unit vectors u_i of 'rV[R]_m and weights c_i > 0 with sum_i c_i u_i^T u_i = 1, and measurable f_i with values in [delta, K], 0 < delta, \int prod_i f_i(<x, u_i>)^(c_i) dgauss_m <= prod_i (\int f_i dnormal_prob 0 1)^(c_i) (real integrals, powR). Proof by the Ornstein-Uhlenbeck semigroup: \int exp (sum_i c_i ln (mehler r f_i)(<x, u_i>)) dgauss_m is nonincreasing in r on [0, 1], equal to the left side at r = 1 and to the right side at r = 0.

Main result, as the README states it

The Gaussian Brascamp-Lieb inequality for bounded functions: for unit vectors u_i of 'rV[R]_m and weights c_i > 0 with sum_i c_i u_i^T u_i = 1, and measurable f_i with values in [delta, K], 0 < delta, \int prod_i f_i(<x, u_i>)^(c_i) dgauss_m <= prod_i (\int f_i dnormal_prob 0 1)^(c_i) (real integrals, powR). Proof by the Ornstein-Uhlenbeck semigroup: \int exp (sum_i c_i ln (mehler r f_i)(<x, u_i>)) dgauss_m is nonincreasing in r on [0, 1], equal to the left side at r = 1 and to the right side at r = 0.
