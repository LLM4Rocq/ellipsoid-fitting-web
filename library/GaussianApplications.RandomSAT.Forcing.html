The clauses have size k.+1 and n.+1 variables, with k < n.

  • binom_mean p m f is the mean of f over a binomial number of successes in m trials of probability p, defined by its recursion on the first trial. binom_rep_sum_le sums the replacement differences rep_diff P g j d = P (j - d) - P (j + (g - 1) d) of a nonincreasing P with values in [0, 1], by telescoping (sum_shift_diff_le).
  • If the first variable is forced by the clauses t, then deleting it, that is dropping the clauses that contain it, leaves a satisfiable formula, and erasing it, that is removing its literal from these clauses, leaves an unsatisfiable one (head_forced_reduction).
  • The number of clauses that contain the first variable is binomial with p = k.+1 / n.+1, and the other clauses are uniform of size k.+1 on the other n variables (del_mean_binom). Erasing, then replacing each shortened clause by a block of g clauses of size k.+1 (kill_replacement of Replacement.v), costs at most a^-k per shortened clause (era_mean_replacement).
  • integrated_head_forcing: for 1 <= k < n, a > 0 with 1 / (n + 1) <= a^-k and g >= 2 (k + 1) a, the probabilities head_forced n k j that the first variable is forced by j clauses satisfy

sum_(j <= M) head_forced n k j <= g M p + (M + 1) M p a^-k.

  • The renamings of the variables preserve the uniform law of the clauses and move the forced variables (forced_rename). Hence the mean fraction of forced variables forced_frac n k m equals head_forced n k m (forced_frac_head).

The manuscript erases the last variable; here, as in the Lean development, the first. The manuscript bounds the binomial weights by (k M / n)^d / d!; here, as in the Lean development, the replacement differences are summed under the binomial mean of the cap M (binom_rep_sum_le), which gives the term g M p instead of the manuscript's (k M / n) e^(k M / n) g; at M = 2^(k+1) n this removes the factor e^(k 2^(k+1)) from the constant.

For 1 <= k < n, a > 0 with 1 / (n + 1) <= a^-k, an integer g >= 2 (k + 1) a and p = (k + 1) / (n + 1), the probabilities h_j that the first variable is forced by j independent uniform clauses of size k + 1 on n + 1 variables satisfy sum_(j <= M) h_j <= g M p + (M + 1) M p a^-k for every M. The mean fraction of forced variables after j clauses is h_j.
