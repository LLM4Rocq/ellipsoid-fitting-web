GaussianApplications

GaussianApplications28 files5,905 lines335 lemmas and theorems, 99 definitions

Rocq formalizations of theorems of manuscripts of the OpenAI math collection (openai/math at revision 1581d4cbd, under the Apache License 2.0) whose proofs reuse the library GaussianAnalysis of library/. The logical root is GaussianApplications.

Guide

Written for this documentation from the sources and the README, and checked against the Rocq statements. The file headers and the statements are the reference.

This directory, with logical root GaussianApplications, formalizes seven theorems, T1 to T7, from five manuscripts of the OpenAI math collection (openai/math at revision 1581d4cbd, families 126, 154, 222, 227 and 235): Gaussian matrix series, tensor packing, the GOE edge, the spectral gap of the SK model, orthogonal splitting of positive semidefinite matrices, the Ising perceptron and random k-SAT. Each header cites its manuscript and, after the marker Lean:, any Lean module it follows.

Everything is over an arbitrary R : realType and requires only MathComp and library/, which holds the general mathematics; this directory holds the statements and the parts specific to a model.

Each target has frozen statement files, XStatement.v and, where needed, XDefinitions.v, and a theorem file XTheorem.v. T1, T2, T3 and T5 follow from library theorems; T4, T6 and T7 have proof folders.

Core definitions

  • lambda_max, goe_mx (library/matrix/lambda_max.v, library/random_matrix/goe.v): the supremum of x^T A x over unit columns x, and the GOE matrix (A + A^T) / sqrt (2 d) with A = vec_mx a.
  • ising_weight, ising_mass, ising_mean, ising_var (SKGapDefinitions.v): for a matrix A and a field theta, the weight exp (x^T A x / 2 + <theta, x>) of a spin configuration x, the normalized law nu, its mean and variance.
  • half_diff, ising_cond_mean, ising_cond_var, ising_dirichlet (SKGapDefinitions.v): the half-difference d_i f, the conditional mean m_i of the spin i given the others, v_i = 1 - m_i^2, and the Dirichlet form E(f) = sum_i nu[v_i (d_i f)^2].
  • ising_cov, sk_coupling (SKGapDefinitions.v): the covariance matrix of the spins, and J = beta (W - Diag W) with W = goe_mx a.
  • ising_dproj, ising_gen (SKGap/Ising.v): D_i = I - E_i, with E_i the conditional expectation given the spins outside i, and L = sum_i D_i.
  • sk_good_disorder (SKGap/Disorder.v): the a such that J = sk_coupling beta a has opnorm J <= 2 beta + delta, sum_j J_ij^2 <= beta^2 + delta for every i, and |J_ij| <= delta.
  • perceptron_pressure, gauss_pressure, sign_pressure, bounded_C3 (PerceptronSignDefinitions.v): p_N(G, f) = (1/N) ln (2^(-N) sum_x exp (sum_mu f ((G x)_mu / sqrt N))) for patterns G in 'M[R]_(M, N), its means under Gaussian and uniform sign patterns, and the potentials f with three derivatives, f''' continuous, |f| <= B0, |f'| <= B1, |f''| <= B2 and |f'''| <= B3.
  • sat_prefix_len, sat_capped_var (SatVarianceDefinitions.v): the largest m <= M such that the first m clauses of a tuple of M proper signed k-clauses are satisfiable together, and its variance for M = 2^(k+1) n independent uniform clauses on n variables.

Main results

T1, Gaussian matrix series. matrix_series_theorem: for symmetric H_1, ..., H_r in 'M[R]_n, S = sum_j H_j H_j, g of law gauss r and q >= 1, E tr (sum_j g_j H_j)^(2q) <= (2q - 1)!! opnorm S^(q-1) tr S (gauss_mx_series_moment). For B_1, ..., B_r in 'M[R]_(m, p) and q >= 1, E opnorm (sum_j g_j B_j) <= ((2q - 1)!! sigma^(2(q-1)) tau)^(1/(2q)), with sigma^2 = max (opnorm (sum_j B_j B_j^T), opnorm (sum_j B_j^T B_j)) and tau = 2 tr (sum_j B_j B_j^T) (gauss_mx_series_opnorm); and E opnorm (sum_j g_j B_j) <= 5 d^(1/4) sqrt (ln (2 + d)) for every real d >= 1 with sigma^2 <= sqrt d and tau <= 2 d (gauss_mx_series_opnorm_log; the statement asks only for some C > 0 in place of 5).

T2, tensor packing. tensor_packing_theorem: for x_a and y_a, a < m, in the unit balls of 'rV[R]_n1 and 'rV[R]_n2, relations Gx and Gy on 'I_m with at most L successors at each vertex containing every pair a != b with |<x_a, x_b>| > eta, respectively |<y_a, y_b>| > eta, Frobenius-orthonormal A_1, ..., A_r in 'M[R]_(n1, n2) with r <= d for an integer d >= 1, 0 < eps <= 1 and 0 <= eta <= eps^2 / 10^4, at most 30000 (1 + L) / eps^2 exp (30000 eps^(-2) sqrt d ln (2 + d)) indices a have sum_k (x_a A_k y_a^T)^2 >= eps^2.

T3, GOE edge. goe_edge_theorem: for d >= 1, a of law gauss (d * d) and W = goe_mx a, a |-> lambda_max W is measurable and integrable, E lambda_max W <= 2, and for every t >= 0, P(lambda_max W >= 2 + t) and P(lambda_max (- W) >= 2 + t) are at most exp (- d t^2 / 4) and P(opnorm W >= 2 + t) <= 2 exp (- d t^2 / 4).

T5, orthogonal splitting. psd_splitting_theorem: for L >= 1 and positive semidefinite A_1, ..., A_L in 'M[R]_n, there are orthogonal projections P_1, ..., P_L, possibly zero, with P_s P_t = 0 for s != t, sum_s P_s = 1 and sum_z sum_(s != z) |P_s A_z P_s|_F^2 <= 2^(L-1) sum_z sum_(z' != z) tr (A_z A_z').

T4, SK spectral gap. sk_gap_theorem: for 0 <= beta < 1/2 and kappa = (1 - 2 beta) / 2, there are measurable sets E_n of 'rV[R]_(n * n) such that, for every eps > 0, gauss (n * n) (E_n) >= 1 - eps for all large n, and for a in E_n, s in [0, 1] and every field theta, the Ising law nu of s (sk_coupling beta a) and theta satisfies kappa Var_nu(f) <= E(f) for every f and opnorm (ising_cov) <= 1 / kappa. The events are sk_good_disorder n beta (sk_gap_tol beta) (sk_good_disorder_prob, sk_gap_deterministic). The key inequality is ising_two_spin: for A symmetric with zero diagonal, i != j and t = tanh A_ij with |t| <= 1/1000, nu[D_i f D_j f] >= - nu[(t + 2 t^2 m_i m_j) v_i v_j d_i f d_j f] - 70000 |t|^3 (nu[v_i (d_i f)^2] + nu[v_j (d_j f)^2]).

T6, perceptron pressure. perceptron_sign_theorem: for bounds B0, ..., B3 and C = 1 + (|B3| + 6 |B1| |B2| + 6 |B1|^3) / 2, for every f with bounded_C3 f B0 B1 B2 B3, every M and N >= 1, g |-> p_N(vec_mx g, f) is integrable against gauss (M * N) and |sign_pressure M N f - gauss_pressure M N f| <= C M N^(-3/2).

T7, random k-SAT. sat_variance_theorem: for k >= 3 there is C > 0 with sat_capped_var R n k <= C n^(1 + 2/k) for every n >= k + 1; sat_variance_bound_explicit gives C = 8 4^(k+1) k (3 k + 2^(k+2)). On the way, sat_prefix_lenE identifies the capped prefix length with the lifetime (life) of the set of all assignments, and by killE_forced one uniform k-clause empties a nonempty set with b forced variables (forced) with probability C(b, k) / (C(n, k) 2^k).

How the proofs go

  • T1: Gaussian integration by parts and a trace inequality give E tr X^(2q+2) <= (2q + 1) opnorm S E tr X^(2q) for symmetric H_j; rectangular matrices go through the symmetric dilation.
  • T2: spectral cuts at sqrt d, coverings, Sudakov's minoration and the last bound of T1.
  • T3: the Sudakov-Fernique inequality on finite families of unit vectors and a net give E lambda_max W <= 2; lambda_max W is sqrt (2 / d)-Lipschitz in a, and Gaussian concentration gives the tails, with a sharper constant than the pi^2 of the TeX and the Lean modules.
  • T5: the spectral projection of A - B onto its nonnegative eigenspaces splits two matrices, then an induction on L relative to an orthogonal projection.
  • T4: SKGap/Disorder.v bounds four failure events with goe_opnorm_edge_tail and Gaussian and chi-square tails. SKGap/TwoSpin.v bounds the remainder in t by a real polynomial certificate (ts_certificate, ts_coef_bound) instead of the manuscript's complex Cauchy estimate. Summing the two-spin inequality over pairs bounds nu[(L f)^2] below by a multiple of E(f) (ising_gen_sq_ge), and the Schur test (opnorm_row_sum) controls the multiple under good disorder; since L is self-adjoint with the constants as kernel, wpoincare_from_square gives the gap.
  • T6: each entry of G enters one coordinate of G x, so log_partition_derive3 bounds the third derivative in each entry by (B3 + 6 B1 B2 + 6 B1^3) / (N^2 sqrt N), and rademacher_gauss_lindeberg replaces the M N entries one at a time (perceptron_sign_compare); the continuity of f''' is not used.
  • T7 follows the route of the Lean development. The law of total variance for the first clause bounds the variance of the lifetime by 2 m times a sum of mean truncated killing probabilities min(M q, 1) (life_var_le_occ), where the manuscript omits one clause. Each is at most a multiple of the fraction of forced variables (trunc_le_forced), whose mean is the probability that the first variable is forced (forced_frac_head); integrated_head_forcing bounds the sum of these probabilities, through kill_replacement.

Connections

The directory requires modules of gaussian/, matrix/, random_matrix/ and probability/, and comparison/gauss_smoothing.v; nothing of convex/ or fourier/. No module of library/, extension/ or theories/ requires it. Only the seven axiom checks of check/, such as check/SKGapAssumptions.v, require its statement and theorem files.

The spectral step of T4 (item T4a). For any matrix A, field theta and real k: if k E(g) <= nu[(L g)^2] for every g, where nu is the Ising law, E = ising_dirichlet A theta and L = ising_gen A theta = sum_i D_i, then k Var_nu(f) <= E(f) for every f.
