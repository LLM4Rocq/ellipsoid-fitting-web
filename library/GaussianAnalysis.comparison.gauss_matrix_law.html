Gaussian matrices from Gaussian rows

A Gaussian matrix of size n x m is vec_mx a for a of law gauss (n * m), as in escape.v and cgmt.v. This file identifies two other descriptions of its law.

  • gauss_tuple_mx: n independent rows of law gauss m, put in a matrix by tuple_mx, give the same law: for X measurable, tuple_prod (fun=> gauss m) n (tuple_mx @^-1` X) is gauss (n * m) [set a | X (vec_mx a)].
  • gauss_trmx: the transpose of a Gaussian matrix of size n x m is a Gaussian matrix of size m x n.

Both follow from gauss_unique: the coordinates are permuted, and the laws agree on boxes by gauss_box and tuple_prod_box.

Two descriptions of the law of an n x m matrix vec_mx a of independent standard normal entries (a of law gauss (n * m)): the matrix tuple_mx t of n independent rows of law gauss m has this law, tuple_prod (fun=> gauss m) n (tuple_mx @^-1` X) = gauss (n * m) [set a | X (vec_mx a)], and so has the transpose of an m x n such matrix, gauss (n * m) [set a | X (vec_mx a)^T] = gauss (m * n) [set b | X (vec_mx b)]. Both by gauss_unique on boxes.
