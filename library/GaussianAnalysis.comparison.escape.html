Let a have law gauss (N * M), so that G = vec_mx a is an N x M matrix of independent standard normal entries, and let g and h have laws gauss N and gauss M. Vectors x of R^M are column vectors 'cV[R]_M, and |x| is frobenius x.

For a nonempty set T of unit vectors of 'cV[R]_M and N > 0, gordon_escape is Gordon's escape theorem: E[inf_(x in T) |G x|] >= E[|g|] - w(T), where w(T) = E[sup_(x in T) <h, x>] is the Gaussian width of T. By Gaussian concentration, inf_(x in T) |G x| is at most E[|g|] - w(T) - u with probability at most expR (- u^2 / 2) (gordon_escape_tail). So when w(T) < E[|g|], the kernel of G misses T with high probability: escape through a mesh. For T the unit sphere, w(T) = E[|h|] (gwidth_sphere), which bounds the smallest singular value of G (gauss_min_singular_mean).

For a second nonempty set Y of unit vectors of 'cV[R]_N, gordon_minmax is Gordon's comparison for the min-max of the bilinear form: E[inf_(x in T) sup_(y in Y) <y, G x>] >= w(Y) - w(T), with w(Y) = E[sup_(y in Y) <g, y>]. Let Y be the unit vectors of the range of an orthogonal projection P != 0 of 'cV[R]_N. Then sup_(y in Y) <y, v> is |P v| (gwidth_range_sphere), so E[inf_(x in T) |P G x|] >= E[|g P|] - w(T) (gordon_projection), with the Gaussian tail of gordon_escape_tail (gordon_projection_tail). Since E[|g A|^2] = |A|^2 (gauss_sqr_mulmx), the Gaussian Poincare inequality gives E[|g P|] >= sqrt (tr P - 1) (gauss_proj_mean_ge). So for M > 0, inf_(|x| = 1) |P G x| is at most sqrt (tr P - 1) - sqrt M - u with probability at most expR (- u^2 / 2) (gauss_proj_singular_tail): the smallest singular value of G seen through P, for instance on the symmetric matrices.

For N, M > 0, E[opnorm G] <= E[|g|] + E[|h|] <= sqrt N + sqrt M (gauss_opnorm_mean, gauss_frobenius_mean_le, gauss_opnorm_mean_sqrt), and opnorm G exceeds E[|g|] + E[|h|] + u with probability at most expR (- u^2 / 2) (gauss_opnorm_tail).

The proofs compare, for finite families xs of unit vectors of 'cV[R]_M and ys of unit vectors of 'cV[R]_N, the matrix process ys t^T *m G *m xs u with the process <g, ys t> + <h, xs u>: by Gordon's inequality for the min-max over u and t (aux_bil_minmax), and by the Sudakov-Fernique inequality for the maximum (bil_aux_max). Nets of T and of the unit spheres (net_exists, unit_sphere_net) pass from finite families to T and to the spheres. The file also proves that gauss n is invariant under x |-> - x (integral_gauss_opp).
