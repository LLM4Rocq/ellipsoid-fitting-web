Lemma 3.10 of Koehler and Sohn (arXiv 2608.27372, lines 2880-2948): for c0 d <= m, q <= C0 d, the m x q matrices of nuclear norm at most gam d are covered by C^(m q) translates of the product of q Euclidean balls of radius gam, one for each column, with C depending on c0 and C0 only. The paper bounds the volume of the nuclear-norm ball by Urysohn's inequality. Here the covering number is bounded in two steps (dual_ball_covering), for G = vec_mx g with g of law gauss (m * q):

  • Sudakov's minoration (sudakov.v) covers the set K by Frobenius balls of radius lam = sqrt (r gam), since E max over K of <G, A> is at most r E opnorm G <= r (sqrt m + sqrt q) (escape.v);
  • the dual Sudakov inequality covers each such ball by products of column balls of radius gam, since E colmax G <= E opnorm G.

The nuclear norm enters only through the duality <A, B> <= r opnorm B, so the results hold for every set K with this property.

  • dual_ball_covering: for m, q > 0, r, gam > 0 and K with <A, B> <= r opnorm B for every A in K and every B, there are at most exp (48 (m + q) r / gam + 33) matrices such that every A in K is within gam of one of them for colmax.
  • product_covering (Lemma 3.10): with r = gam d, d > 0, c0 > 0 and c0 d <= m, q <= C0 d, at most exp ((96 C0 + 33) / c0^2) ^ (m q) of them.
  • matrix_small_ball, the covering step of Lemma 3.14 (lines 3498-3548): for distinct coordinates y_(g i j), i < n, j < q, of a tuple y of independent coordinates, and w_i in 'rV_m, if every column sum sum_i y_(g i j) w_i is within gam of any given vector with probability at most p, then colsum_mx g w y + B is in a measurable K as above with probability at most exp (48 (m + q) r / gam + 33) p^q: cover K, then use the independence of the columns.

Main result, as the README states it

Lemma 3.10 of Koehler and Sohn: if c0 d <= m, q <= C0 d with c0 > 0 and d > 0, the m x q matrices of nuclear norm at most gam d (written by duality: <A, B> <= gam d opnorm B for every B) are covered by exp ((96 C0 + 33) / c0^2) ^ (m q) translates of the product of the column balls of radius gam (the ball of colmax, the largest Euclidean norm of a column). Proved by Sudakov's minoration and the dual Sudakov inequality instead of Urysohn's inequality.

Small-ball probability of a random matrix with independent columns. The entries y_(g i j) are distinct coordinates of a tuple y of law tuple_prod mu N. If, for every column j, the sum sum_i y_(g i j) w_i is within gam of any given vector with probability at most p, then the matrix colsum_mx g w y + B lies in a measurable set K with <A, C> <= r opnorm C on K with probability at most exp (48 (m + q) r / gam + 33) p^q: cover K by that many products of column balls (dual_ball_covering) and use the independence of the columns.
