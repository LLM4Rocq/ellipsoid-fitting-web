Theorem 5.1 of Adamczak, Litvak, Pajor and Tomczak-Jaegermann (Constr. Approx. 2011, arXiv 0904.4723) at r = 1, with explicit constants: the off-diagonal part of the Gram matrix of independent centered psi_1 vectors is small on sparse vectors. Koehler and Sohn (arXiv 2608.27372, lines 2192-2221) cite it for the a priori estimates of their Proposition 3.1. With it, the norms of sparse combinations of independent subgaussian vectors (their second a priori estimate).

Setting: x_1, ..., x_N in 'rV[R]_n are the coordinates of x of law tuple_prod mu N, and every linear form <x_i, y>, |y| = 1, is centered and subexponential with constant psi under mu i. The rows of tuple_mx x are the x_i, so u *m tuple_mx x = sum_i u_i x_i.

Main results:

  • restricted_isometry: for 1 <= m <= N, 0 < beta <= 1/2 and K >= 1, there is a measurable event G with P(not G) <= 2 (log_4 m + 2) exp (- K sqrt m ln (e N / m)) on which B_m^2 <= 256 T^2 + 32 T C + 8 beta C^2, T = rip_T beta K and C = maxnorm x. Hence |u X|^2 - sum_i u_i^2 |x_i|^2 is at most this bound times |u|^2 for every m-sparse u (gram_off_rip).
  • sparse_isometry_dev: | |u X|^2 - |u|^2 | <= (B_m^2 + sqnorm_dev x) |u|^2 for every m-sparse u, which bounds the restricted isometry constant.
  • tuple_mx_blocks: if |u X| <= L |u| for every m-sparse u, then |u X| <= L sqrt (N %/ m + 1) |u| for every u (blocks of m indices).
  • sparse_norm_subgaussian: if the linear forms of the x_i are centered and subgaussian with constant K, then with probability at least 1 - 2 exp (- s), |u X| <= sparse_norm_bound m s |u| for every m-sparse u: Hoeffding's inequality for a fixed pair (tuple_mx_dot_tail), nets of both unit spheres (tuple_submx_net) and a union bound over the supports.

The proof of restricted_isometry, after ALPT with value levels instead of sorted coordinates:

  • cross_tail: for a and b with disjoint supports, |a| <= 1 and |a_i| <= al, P(|<a X, b X>| > t |b X|) <= 2 exp (- min (t^2 / (64 psi^2), t / (4 al psi))), by Bernstein's inequality conditionally on the vectors in the support of b (tuple_prod_cond_le of conditioning.v);
  • gram_off_decouple: the off-diagonal form is an average of decoupled forms <restr E y X, restr (F \ E) y X> over the subsets E of F;
  • the levels of z: level k collects the coordinates with 4^(k-1) < m z_i^2 <= 4^k; it has at most lsize k elements, each at most alpha k = 2^k / sqrt m (level_size, level_alpha), and z is the sum of its restrictions to the levels (level_sum);
  • sparse_net: nets of the vectors supported in F with sup norm at most al, of size exponential in |F|; small_sets_le: there are at most (e N / s)^s sets of size at most s;
  • rip_const_le: if every decoupled diagonal term of level k and every term of level k against the levels above (upper) is at most t k times the norm of its second vector, then B_m^2 <= 256 T^2 + 32 T C + 8 beta C^2 with T = sum_k t k (the approximation by nets, the triangular expansion gram_off_tri and the bound A_m^2 <= B_m^2 + C^2 in place of the A_m of ALPT);
  • bad_event_le, conf_size, tk_exp: the union bound over the finite lists wconf k and xconf k of configurations, whose sizes are at most 2 exp (2 H_k) with H_k = (4 m / 4^k) (9 + ln (N / m) - ln beta + 3 k) (ccount_le); tk_sum: the thresholds t k sum to at most rip_T beta K.

Main result, as the README states it

Norms of sparse combinations of independent subgaussian vectors: if x_1, ..., x_N in 'rV[R]_n are independent (tuple_prod mu N) with every linear form <x_i, y>, |y| = 1, centered and subgaussian with constant K, then for 1 <= m <= N and s >= 0, outside a measurable event of probability at most 2 exp (- s), every m-sparse u satisfies |sum_i u_i x_i| <= 8 K sqrt (m (1 + ln (N / m)) + 6 (m + n) + s) |u| (Vershynin's Theorem 4.4.5 with a union bound over the supports).
