The Brascamp-Lieb inequality: from bounded functions to [0, +oo]

Let u_1, ..., u_n be unit vectors of 'rV[R]_m and c_1, ..., c_n positive weights such that sum_i c_i u_i^T u_i is the identity matrix. The Gaussian form of the Brascamp-Lieb inequality for measurable f_i with values in [delta, K], 0 < delta,

int prod_i f_i(<x, u_i>)^{c_i} dgauss_m(x)
  <= prod_i (int f_i d(normal_prob 0 1))^{c_i},

is gauss_brascamp_lieb_bounded of brascamp_lieb.v. This file derives:

  • the same for measurable f_i with values in [0, K] (gauss_brascamp_lieb_ge0): apply it to f_i + 1/(k+1), and let k -> oo; the right side converges since a^c is continuous on [0, +oo) for c > 0;
  • the same for measurable F_i : R -> [0, +oo] (gauss_brascamp_lieb), with extended integrals and poweR: apply the previous case to the truncations min (F_i, k), monotone convergence on the left, and monotonicity on the right; the product of the truncated factors converges to the product of the limits, with 0 * +oo = 0;
  • the Lebesgue form (brascamp_lieb)

    int_{R^m} prod_i F_i(<x, u_i>)^{c_i} dx <= prod_i (int_R F_i)^{c_i},

    from the Gaussian form for f_i(t) = F_i(t) sqrt (2 pi) exp (t^2 / 2): the integral of f_i against the standard normal law is the Lebesgue integral of F_i, and since sum_i c_i <x, u_i>^2 = |x|^2 and sum_i c_i = m (tight_frame_mxdot_sqr, tight_frame_sum), prod_i (sqrt (2 pi) exp (<x, u_i>^2 / 2))^{c_i} = (2 pi)^{m/2} exp (|x|^2 / 2) is the inverse of the density gauss_pdf of gauss m with respect to lebesgue_rV m.

ResultsStatement
brascamp_lieb
The Brascamp-Lieb inequality in Lebesgue form (Ball's geometric form): for unit vectors u_i of 'rV[R]_m and weights c_i > 0 with sum_i c_i u_i^T u_i = 1, and measurable F_i : R -> [0, +oo], \int prod_i F_i(<x, u_i>)^(c_i) dx <= prod_i (\int F_i)^(c_i) (lebesgue_rV m on the left, poweR powers). Proved from gauss_brascamp_lieb applied to F_i(t) sqrt (2 pi) exp (t^2 / 2), with the density gauss_pdf of gauss m.
gauss_brascamp_lieb
The Gaussian form for measurable F_i : R -> [0, +oo]: \int prod_i F_i(<x, u_i>)^(c_i) dgauss_m <= prod_i (\int F_i dnormal_prob 0 1)^(c_i), in extended reals with poweR. From gauss_brascamp_lieb_ge0 applied to the truncations min (F_i, k): monotone convergence on the left (the product of the nondecreasing truncated factors converges to the product of the limits, with 0 * +oo = 0), monotonicity on the right.
