Theorem 2.9 of Koehler and Sohn (arXiv 2608.27372, lines 1282-1344, proof lines 1637-2116 and 3809-4225), in the paper's abstract feature form, for two laws mu and nu of features on 'rV[R]_N that are (K, tau)-regular with the same covariance Sigma (regular_feature of least_squares.v), with lambda = opnorm Sigma and tau >= 0. D is nonempty, convex and relatively open with directions V (rel_open_convex), B >= 0 is a self-concordant function on D (sc_function), beta, gamma > 0 and n > 0. Phi Z is the value ls_barrier_value Z lambda beta D gamma B of the features Z, and

Lref = (1 + 1 / beta) (1 + inf_(theta in D) (lambda |theta|^2
       + gamma B theta))

is ls_ref_scale lambda beta gamma D B. The paper asks D to be bounded and B to be a barrier; the proof uses neither.

hybrid mu nu kthe laws of the coordinates of the Lindeberg argument: mu before the coordinate k, nu from k on
  • barrier_lindeberg_mean, (2.12): if 36 <= n gamma, then
    |E_(mu^n) Phi - E_(nu^n) Phi| <= 100 (1 + K^4)^2 Lref^2
  ((n gamma)^(-1/2) + sqrt (dim V) (tau / lambda + 1) / n).
  • barrier_lindeberg_variance, (2.13): Var_(mu^n) Phi <= 4 (1 + K^4)^2 Lref^2 / n.
  • barrier_lindeberg_concentration, (2.14): for t > 0 and 36 <= n gamma, Phi is within the bound of (2.12) plus t of E_(nu^n) Phi, except on an event of mu^n-probability at most 4 (1 + K^4)^2 Lref^2 / (n t^2).

The hypotheses are symmetric in mu and nu, so the last two hold for nu.

The proof is the paper's. measurable_ls_barrier_value: Phi is a measurable function of the features (an infimum over a countable dense subset of D). lindeberg_telescope: the replacement argument for an integrable functional with bounds that depend on the other coordinates. insertion_compare (Propositions 2.14 and 2.16): the comparison of the means of an insertion cost under mu and nu, from its pointwise bounds (ls_insertion.v) and the moment calculus of feature_moments.v; le_amgm turns the Cauchy-Schwarz step into an infimum over a weight. loo_compare and loo_variance: the same for the value, at the leave-one-out minimizer of minimizers.v.
