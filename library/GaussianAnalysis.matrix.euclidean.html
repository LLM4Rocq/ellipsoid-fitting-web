The Frobenius inner product and norm of rectangular matrices, and orthogonal matrices. On row and column vectors, the Frobenius inner product and norm are the Euclidean inner product and norm.

mxdot A Bthe Frobenius inner product of A, B : 'M[R]_(m, n), \sum_i \sum_j A i j * B i j
frobenius Athe Frobenius norm Num.sqrt (mxdot A A) of A; the Euclidean norm when A is a row or a column vector
M \is orthogonalmxthe square matrix M is orthogonal: M *m M^T = 1
