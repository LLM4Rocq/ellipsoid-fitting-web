The moments of the standard normal law follow from Stein's identity: E[X^(k+2)] = (k + 1) E[X^k] (normal_momentS), so the odd moments vanish and E[X^(2k)] = 1 * 3 * ... * (2k - 1) (normal_moment_odd, normal_moment_even). A vector x of law gauss n has mean 0 and covariance the identity (gauss_mean, gauss_cov), and x *m A has covariance A^T *m A (gauss_mulmx_cov).
