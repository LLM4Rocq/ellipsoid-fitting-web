Effective rank of a weighted sum of rank-one matrices

Let u_1, ..., u_n be row vectors of R^p whose rank-one matrices u_i^T u_i are close to the identity in the sense that

sum_i <u_i^T u_i - I, A>^2 <= L^2 |A|_F^2   for every p x p matrix A,

and let t be weights with |t| = 1 and 8 L^2 t_i^2 <= k for every i, with 0 < k <= p. Then Sigma_t = sum_i t_i^2 u_i^T u_i has fewer than k eigenvalues below 1/8: its sorted eigenvalues spec Sigma_t (von_neumann.v) are at least 1/8 at every index j with j + k <= p (effective_rank_spec).

This is the deterministic part of Lemma 3.12 of Koehler and Sohn (arXiv 2608.27372, lines 3314-3405 of arxiv_v1.tex), where the hypothesis comes from the a priori estimate of the features (u_i u_i^T - I) / sqrt p. The proof is the paper's: if k eigenvalues were below 1/8, the projection H on their eigenvectors would give <H, Sigma_t> < k / 8, while the hypothesis at A = H, which has |H|_F^2 = tr H = k, bounds the weight of the points with u_i H u_i^T < k / 2, and gives <H, Sigma_t> >= k / 4.

effective_rank_spec
Effective rank of a weighted sum of rank-one matrices (the deterministic part of Lemma 3.12 of Koehler and Sohn): if u_1, ..., u_n in 'rV_p satisfy sum_i <u_i^T u_i - I, A>^2 <= L^2 |A|_F^2 for every A, and |t| = 1 with 8 L^2 t_i^2 <= k for every i, 0 < k <= p, then Sigma_t = sum_i t_i^2 u_i^T u_i has fewer than k eigenvalues below 1/8: spec Sigma_t j >= 1/8 whenever j + k <= p.
