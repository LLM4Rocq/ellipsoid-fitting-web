MathComp-Analysis defines normal_prob m s as the measure with density normal_pdf m s against Lebesgue measure. This file turns that definition into a formula for integrals (integral_normal_prob) and proves that the powers, hence the functions of polynomial growth, are integrable against the standard normal law, with mean 0.

Main results

ResultsStatement
integral_normal_probintegrable_normal_prob
Integrating against normal_prob m s is integrating against Lebesgue measure with the density normal_pdf m s, for nonnegative or integrable functions.
integrable_normal_exprnintegrable_normal_poly
Every power |x|^k, hence every measurable function bounded by C (1 + |x|^k), is integrable against the standard normal law.
