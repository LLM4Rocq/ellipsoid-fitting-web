A tuple of n * d coordinates read as n rows of d coordinates, row i being made of the coordinates i * d + j for j < d, as the samples of the ellipsoid fitting papers are read.

row_index i jthe index i * d + j of 'I_(n * d), for i : 'I_n and j : 'I_d
tuple_rows n d xthe n rows of length d of the (n * d)-tuple x
tuple_map g x[tuple g i (tnth x i) | i < n], for x : n.-tuple T and g : nat -> T -> T'

Main results:

  • ge0_integral_tuple_prodS, integral_tuple_prodS: Fubini for the first coordinate, \int[tuple_prod mu n.+1] F = \int[mu 0]_t \int[tuple_prod (fun i => mu i.+1) n]_ts F (t :: ts), for F nonnegative and measurable, or integrable (integrable_tuple_prodS); the lemmas of lindeberg.v state it for bounded F;
  • ge0_integral_tuple_prod_prod: independent coordinates, the integral of \prod_i f_i (x_i) against tuple_prod mu n is the product of the integrals of the f_i against the mu i, for f_i nonnegative and measurable;
  • ge0_integral_image: the integral against a law mu that is the image of nu under a measurable g is the integral of f \o g against nu;
  • coordinatewise maps preserve independence: if mu i is the image of nu i under a measurable g i for every i, then tuple_prod mu n is the image of tuple_prod nu n under tuple_map g (tuple_prod_map), so that the integral of a measurable G against tuple_prod mu n is the integral of x |-> G (tuple_map g x) against tuple_prod nu n (ge0_integral_tuple_map, integral_tuple_map, Rintegral_tuple_map), and integrable_tuple_map;
  • blocks: Fubini for the first a and the last b coordinates, \int[tuple_prod mu (a + b)] F = \int[tuple_prod mu a]_x \int[tuple_prod (fun i => mu (a + i)) b]_y F [tuple of x ++ y] (ge0_integral_tuple_prod_cat), so disjoint blocks are independent with the product laws of their coordinates;
  • rows: the image of tuple_prod mu (n * m) under tuple_rows n m is the product over i < n of the laws tuple_prod (fun j => mu (i * m + j)) m of the rows (tuple_prod_rows, ge0_integral_tuple_rows, integral_tuple_rows);
  • eq_tuple_prod: tuple_prod mu n depends only on mu 0, ..., mu (n - 1).
