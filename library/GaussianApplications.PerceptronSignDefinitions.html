The definitions of the statement of PerceptronSignStatement.v, from 'The free energy of the Ising random perceptron' (family 222 of the OpenAI math collection, revision 1581d4cbd, sections/model.tex, equation def:pressure, and sections/extensions.tex, Theorem ext:sign). The collection has no Lean version of it. R is any realType.

  • spin_col x is the column of the spins of x in {-1, 1}^N, x of {ffun 'I_N -> bool}.
  • perceptron_pressure f G = (1/N) ln (2^(-N) sum_x exp (sum_mu f ((G x)_mu / sqrt N))), for patterns G of 'M[R]_(M, N), with rows g^1, ..., g^M, and a potential f : R -> R: the normalized pressure p_N(f) of the manuscript, with the uniform probability on the cube and b_mu(x) = <g^mu, x> / sqrt N, at M patterns.
  • gauss_pressure M N f is its expectation under Gaussian patterns: G = vec_mx g, with g of law gauss (M * N), so that the M N entries of G are independent standard normal. sign_pressure M N f is its expectation under sign patterns: the average over the 2^(M N) matrices of entries in {-1, 1}, so that the entries are independent uniform signs.
  • bounded_C3 f B0 B1 B2 B3: f has derivatives f', f'' and f''' everywhere, f''' is continuous (f is of class C^3), and |f| <= B0, |f'| <= B1, |f''| <= B2, |f'''| <= B3 on R.

Definition bounded_C3 (f : R -> R) (B0 B1 B2 B3 : R) : Prop :=
  exists f1 f2 f3 : R -> R,
  [/\ forall x : R, is_derive x 1 f (f1 x),
      forall x : R, is_derive x 1 f1 (f2 x),
      forall x : R, is_derive x 1 f2 (f3 x),
      continuous f3 &
      forall x : R,
        [/\ `|f x| <= B0, `|f1 x| <= B1, `|f2 x| <= B2 & `|f3 x| <= B3]].
