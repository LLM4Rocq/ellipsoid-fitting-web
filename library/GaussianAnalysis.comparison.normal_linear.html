Linear images of normal laws

Lebesgue measure on the line, pushed forward by x |-> a x + b with a != 0, is |a|^-1 times Lebesgue measure (lebesgue_measure_affine, ge0_integral_lebesgue_affine). Hence the image of normal_prob m s under x |-> a x + b is normal_prob (a m + b) (a s) (normal_prob_affine), and a linear combination a X + b Y of independent normal variables X and Y, with b != 0, is normal (normal_prob_lincomb): its law is normal_prob (a m1 + b m2) (sqrt (a^2 s1^2 + b^2 s2^2)) when X and Y have laws normal_prob m1 s1 and normal_prob m2 s2. The proof computes the convolution of the two densities (normal_pdf_conv).

Laws are compared on measurable sets only: normal_prob m s A is the Lebesgue integral of normal_pdf m s over A, which MathComp-Analysis defines for every set A.
