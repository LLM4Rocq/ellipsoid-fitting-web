The mixture of probabilities P s, s : nat, with weights p s >= 0 of sum one, and the sums over the tuples of labels that describe n independent draws from it. The weights are a proof argument of the mixture, as in probability/mixture.v.

cmixture_weights pp s >= 0 for every s and \sum_(s <oo) p s = 1
cmixture P hpfor hp : cmixture_weights p, the probability A |-> \sum_(s <oo) p s P s (A)
cmixture_tail p mr_m = \sum_(s > m) p s, the weight of the labels above m
cmixture_tail_w p m sp s / r_m for s > m and 0 otherwise, the weights of the normalized tail
labels_le n mthe n-tuples of labels in nat that are all <= m

Main results:

  • cmixtureE: cmixture P hp A = \sum_(s <oo) p s P s (A); the mixture is the measure series mseries of the scaled probabilities p s P s;
  • ge0_integral_cmixture, integral_cmixture: \int f d(cmixture P hp) = \sum_s p s \int f dP_s for f nonnegative and measurable, and for f integrable under every P s with \sum_s p s \int |f| dP_s < +oo, which is then integrable under the mixture (from ge0_integral_measure_series and integral_measure_series);
  • cmixture_tailE, cmixture_tail_small, cmixture_tail_series, cmixture_tail_weights: \sum_(s <= m) p s + r_m = 1, r_m tends to 0, and for r_m > 0 the normalized tail weights are mixture weights, so that cmixture P with them is the normalized tail \sum_(s > m) p s P s / r_m;
  • esumZl, esum_fin, esum_tuple_distr, esum_tuple_prod: an esum of nonnegative terms commutes with a nonnegative factor; over a subset of a finite type it is a finite sum; if the esum of a_i over nat is a real number x_i for every i < n, the esum over s : n.-tuple nat of \prod_i a_i (s_i) is \prod_i x_i (the countable big_tuple_distr), so it is x^n when every a_i is a;
  • label_esum1, label_esum_le: the product weights \prod_i p (s_i) of the label tuples have sum one, and sum (1 - r_m)^n over labels_le n m;
  • label_coupling, bernoulli_tail: an esum over the label tuples in nat and a finite sum over the label tuples in 'I_N, of product weights times probabilities, whose weights and probabilities agree on the tuples with all labels at most m, differ by at most 1 - (1 - r_m)^n <= n r_m on every measurable set;
  • tuple_prod_cmixture: n independent draws from a countable mixture: on every measurable set A of n-tuples, tuple_prod (fun=> cmixture P hp) n A is the esum over the label tuples s : n.-tuple nat of \prod_i p (s_i) times tuple_prod (fun k => P (s_k)) n A (the countable form of tuple_prod_mixture; both sides are measures that agree on boxes, the right one written as a countable mixture over nat through pickle).
