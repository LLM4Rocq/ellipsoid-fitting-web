Part of target T4 of phase 12 (AGENTS.md, section Phase 12). A Rocq formalization of the credited argument of the lemma 'Uniform two-spin inequality' of 'Cutoff throughout the high-temperature Sherrington-Kirkpatrick phase' (family 227 of the OpenAI math collection, revision 1581d4cbd, appendices/entropy.tex). The remainder of the expansion in t is bounded by a real polynomial certificate instead of the manuscript's complex Cauchy estimate, as the Lean module of the collection does:

Lean: pair_cubic_signed_bound (SKRatio/Calculus/TwoSpin.lean)

The coefficients of the certificate are those of the unique polynomial R with t^3 R equal to the numerator of C - L; they were computed independently and agree with those of the Lean module. Nothing is copied from the Lean code.

  • The pair law: for |a|, |b| <= 1 and |t| <= 1/4, plaw a b t F is the mean of F under the law proportional to (1 + a x)(1 + b y)(1 + t x y) on {-1, 1}^2; pcm and pcv are the conditional means and variances, and pg1, pg2 the half-differences p + r (y - b) and q + r (x - a).
  • ts_certificate, ts_coef_bound: the coefficient C of the manuscript is the leading term L = 1 + a b t + (1 - a^2 - b^2 + 2 a^2 b^2) t^2 up to 30000 |t|^3.
  • pair_factorization: the mixed energy plus the correction term is P_0 Q, as in the manuscript; pair_signed_mean_lower: Q >= - 30000 |t|^3 (p^2 + q^2) for |t| <= 1/1000.
  • plaw_energy_ge, pprefactor_bounds: the energies are at least (3/5) (1 - a^2) p^2 and (3/5) (1 - b^2) q^2, and 0 <= P_0 <= (4/3) (1 - a^2) (1 - b^2).
  • pair_two_spin: the inequality of the lemma for the pair law, with t_0 = 1/1000 and C_0 = 70000 (the manuscript's example is t_0 = 1/100, C_0 = 22000; the Lean theorem has C_0 = 1920000).
  • ising_two_spin: the inequality of the lemma for the Ising law of a symmetric A with zero diagonal, for i != j with |tanh A_ij| <= 1/1000: nu[D_i f D_j f] >= - nu[(t + 2 t^2 m_i m_j) v_i v_j d_i f d_j f] - 70000 |t|^3 (nu[v_i (d_i f)^2] + nu[v_j (d_j f)^2]), with t = tanh A_ij. The proof conditions on the spins outside i and j (ising_mean_block, ising_weight_block).

pair_two_spinising_two_spin
The uniform two-spin inequality. For the pair law proportional to (1 + a x)(1 + b y)(1 + t x y) with |a|, |b| <= 1 and |t| <= 1/1000: the mixed energy plus the correction term (t + 2 t^2 m_i m_j) v_i v_j d_i f d_j f is at least - 70000 |t|^3 times the sum of the two energies (pair_two_spin; the remainder in t by a real polynomial certificate, ts_coef_bound).
