Target T4 of phase 12 (AGENTS.md, section Phase 12; the plan notes/phase12-plan.md, section 3). The definitions of the statement of SKGapStatement.v, from 'Cutoff throughout the high-temperature Sherrington-Kirkpatrick phase' (family 227 of the OpenAI math collection, revision 1581d4cbd): sections/02-model-inputs.tex, subsection 'Two clocks and the Dirichlet form', and appendices/entropy.tex, subsection 'A two-spin estimate and a uniform gap'. The Lean counterparts, in lean/OAI/Probability/SKRatio of the collection:

Lean: weight, mass, expectation, variance, dirichlet (Matrices/Spin.lean)

Lean: spinGradient (Calculus/Expectation.lean)

All are finite sums over the cube; R is any realType.

  • The cube {-1, 1}^n is {ffun 'I_n -> bool}. spin b is 1 for true and -1 for false, and spin_vec x is the column of the spins of x. spin_set x i b sets the spin i of x to b: the manuscript's x^(i,+) and x^(i,-) are spin_set x i true and spin_set x i false.
  • The Ising law nu of a matrix A of 'M[R]_n and a field theta of 'cV[R]_n: ising_weight A theta x = exp (x^T A x / 2 + <theta, x>), ising_mass A theta x is ising_weight A theta x divided by the sum of the weights, ising_mean A theta f = sum_x nu(x) f(x), and ising_var A theta f = nu[(f - nu[f])^2]. The statement takes A = s J with J symmetric of zero diagonal, as the manuscript does; the definitions make sense for every A.
  • half_diff i f x = (f (x^(i,+)) - f (x^(i,-))) / 2 is the half-difference d_i f of the manuscript.
  • ising_cond_mean A theta i x = (w (x^(i,+)) - w (x^(i,-))) / (w (x^(i,+)) + w (x^(i,-))), with w = ising_weight A theta, is the conditional mean m_i of the spin i given the other spins under nu; it is tanh ((A x)_i + theta_i) when A is symmetric and A_ii = 0. ising_cond_var A theta i x = 1 - m_i^2 is v_i, the conditional variance of the spin i.
  • ising_dirichlet A theta f = sum_i nu[v_i (d_i f)^2] is the Dirichlet form of the heat-bath dynamics with rate one at every site (the manuscript's equation model-dirichlet); for a law of full support, such as nu, it is sum_i nu[Var(f | x_(-i))], the form of the Lean module.
  • ising_cov A theta is the covariance matrix of the spins under nu: its entry (i, j) is nu[x_i x_j] - nu[x_i] nu[x_j].
  • sk_coupling beta a = beta (W - Diag W), with W = goe_mx a (random_matrix/goe.v). For a of law gauss (n * n), its entries J_ij, i < j, are independent centered normal of variance beta^2 / n, with J_ji = J_ij and J_ii = 0: the disorder of the manuscript.
