A real random variable X on a probability space P is subgaussian with constant K > 0 when E exp (X^2 / K^2) <= 2, and subexponential with constant L > 0 when E exp (|X| / L) <= 2. The least such constants are the norms psi_2 and psi_1 of Vershynin, High-Dimensional Probability, sections 2.5 and 2.7. Independent variables are functions f_i (x_i) of the coordinates of a tuple x of law tuple_prod mu n (lindeberg.v).

subgaussian P X K0 < K, X is measurable and E exp (X^2 / K^2) <= 2
subexponential P X L0 < L, X is measurable and E exp (|X| / L) <= 2
psi2_norm P Xthe infimum of the K with subgaussian P X K

Main results, with explicit constants:

  • subgaussian_tail: P(|X| >= u) <= 2 exp (- u^2 / K^2) for u >= 0; subexponential_tail: P(|Y| >= u) <= 2 exp (- u / L);
  • subgaussian_moment: E X^(2k) <= 2 k! K^(2k), and every power of X is integrable (integrable_subgaussian_exprn);
  • subgaussian_mgf: if E X = 0, then E exp (l X) <= exp (3 l^2 K^2) for every real l;
  • subgaussian_of_mgf: conversely, if E exp (l X) <= exp (l^2 s^2) for every l, then X is subgaussian with every constant L > 0 such that 16 s^2 <= 3 L^2, by integrating against a standard normal law;
  • Hoeffding's inequality, for x of law tuple_prod mu n and centered f_i (x_i), subgaussian with constants K_i (subgaussian_sum_mgf, subgaussian_sum): E exp (l \sum_i a_i f_i (x_i)) <= exp (3 l^2 \sum_i a_i^2 K_i^2), and the sum is subgaussian with constant 4 (\sum_i a_i^2 K_i^2)^(1/2); for laws nu i of the line with mean 0, subgaussian with a common constant K (subgaussian_lincomb), E exp ((\sum_i a_i x_i)^2 / (16 K^2 \sum_i a_i^2)) <= 2 under tuple_prod nu n;
  • subexponential_sqr, subexponential_mul: X^2 is subexponential with constant K^2, and X Y with constant K L, for X, Y subgaussian with constants K, L; subexponential_center: Y - E Y is subexponential with constant 3 L when Y is with constant L; a subexponential Y is integrable (integrable_subexponential);
  • subexponential_mgf: if E Y = 0, then E exp (l Y) <= exp (16 l^2 L^2) for |l| <= 1 / (2 L);
  • Bernstein's inequality, for x of law tuple_prod mu n and centered f_i (x_i), subexponential with constants L_i, and reals a_i (subexponential_sum_mgf, bernstein_upper, bernstein): P(|\sum_i a_i f_i (x_i)| >= t) <= 2 exp (- min (t^2 / (64 s^2), t / (4 M))), where s^2 = \sum_i a_i^2 L_i^2 and M = \max_i |a_i| L_i; bernstein_sum: if every f_i has constant L, P(|\sum_(i < n) f_i (x_i)| >= t) <= 2 exp (- min (t^2 / (64 n L^2), t / (4 L))).

The scalar inequalities behind them: expR_le_quad (e^y <= 1 + y + y^2 e^|y|), exprn_le_expR (u^k <= k! e^u for u >= 0), le_expR34, expR_mean_le and expR_third_le; lee_prod_ge0 is the monotonicity of products of nonnegative extended reals.
