Two classical entropy bounds for Gaussian processes, with explicit constants, and the facts they rest on. g has law gauss n.

  • gauss_max_lower: the largest of n independent standard normal variables has mean at least sqrt (ln n) / 2 - 2.
  • sudakov_minoration (Sudakov): if the points xs i of 'rV[R]_n, i < k + 1, are pairwise at distance at least eps, then eps (sqrt (ln (k + 1)) / 2 - 2) <= sqrt 2 E max_i <g, xs i>. Proof: the Sudakov-Fernique inequality (slepian.v) against independent normal variables of variance eps^2 / 2, then gauss_max_lower.
  • gauss_shift_sym: for a measurable symmetric set A, gauss n (h + A) >= exp (- |h|^2 / 2) gauss n A, by the Cameron-Martin formula (gauss_shift.v) and cosh >= 1.
  • dual_sudakov (Pajor and Tomczak-Jaegermann, by Talagrand's argument): if nrm is a measurable seminorm with E nrm(g) <= L, the points of the Euclidean ball of radius r that are pairwise more than eps apart for nrm are at most 2 exp (8 r^2 L^2 / eps^2).
  • separated_net: if the eps-separated lists of points of a set K have at most M points, K has an eps-net of at most M of its own points, a separated list of largest size.

These two bounds replace volume arguments: covering.v covers the nuclear-norm ball by products of column balls with them.
