Under the product law tuple_prod mu n of n independent coordinates, the coordinates outside a set S may be resampled independently of those in S (ge0_integral_tuple_prod_merge). This gives, without conditional expectations, the tools of a proof that reveals the coordinates in stages, as the proof of Proposition 3.4 of Koehler and Sohn (arXiv 2608.27372, lines 3610-3805) does. An event E depends only on the coordinates in S when replacing the others does not change it: E (tuple_merge S x y) = E x for all x and y.

  • tuple_prod_box, measure_tuple_box_unique: the measure of a box is the product of the measures of its sides, and two finite measures on the n-tuples that agree on the boxes agree.
  • tuple_prod_merge: resampling the coordinates outside S does not change tuple_prod mu n.
  • tuple_prod_cond_le: if H depends only on the coordinates in S and, for every x in H, resampling the coordinates of x outside S puts it in E with probability at most c x, then P(E and H) <= int_H c.
  • tuple_prod_indepS, tuple_prod_indep_big: events that depend on pairwise disjoint sets of coordinates are independent.
  • tuple_prod_chain_le: if the coordinates are revealed in nested sets S 0, S 1, ..., the event A l depends only on the coordinates in S l.+1 and, given the coordinates in S l of a tuple of the earlier events, has probability at most c l, then the A l, l < L, occur together with probability at most the product of the c l.
  • tuple_prod_pick: for an injective f, the coordinates f 0, ..., f (k - 1) of tuple_prod mu n have the product law of their laws.

Main result, as the README states it

For an injective f : 'I_k -> 'I_n, the coordinates f 0, ..., f (k - 1) of tuple_prod mu n have the law tuple_prod nu k with nu i = mu (f i). The measure of a box is the product of the measures of its sides, and two finite measures on n.-tuple T that agree on the boxes agree.
