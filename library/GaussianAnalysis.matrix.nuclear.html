The nuclear norm of A : 'M[R]_(m, n) is the dual norm of the operator norm for the Frobenius pairing <A, B> = mxdot A B. For a symmetric matrix it is the sum of the absolute values of the eigenvalues.

  • nuclear_ub, nuclear_le: the supremum; nuclear_ge0, nuclearZ, nuclearN, nuclearD and nuclear_eq0: a norm; mxdot_le_nuclear, the duality inequality |<A, B>| <= opnorm B * nuclear A.
  • frobenius_le_nuclear and nuclear_le_frobenius: frobenius A <= nuclear A <= sqrt n * frobenius A (frobenius_le_opnorm: frobenius B <= sqrt n * opnorm B); frobenius_sqr_le_nuclear: |A|_F^2 <= opnorm A * nuclear A.
  • nuclear_outer: nuclear (x x^T) = |x|^2.
  • nuclear_spectral: for A = P^T diag(d) P with P orthogonal, nuclear A = sum_i |d_i|, attained at P^T diag(sign d_i) P; nuclear_sym: for A symmetric, the supremum may be taken over the symmetric B.
  • range_duality, Lemma 2.2 of Koehler and Sohn (arXiv 2608.27372, lines 794-826) in the form the paper uses: for W_1, ..., W_k in 'M[R]_n, if |<l, y>| <= M nuclear (coef_adj W l) for every l in 'rV[R]_k, then y = coef_map W D for some D with opnorm D <= M; range_duality_sym: D is symmetric when the W_i are. The proof projects y on the compact convex set {coef_map W D | opnorm D <= M} (proj of library/convex/moreau.v), seen in 'rV[R]_(n * n) through vec_mx (mxdot_vec_mxr): no Slater condition or general convex duality is needed.
nuclear Asup of <A, B> over the B with opnorm B <= 1
coef_map W Dthe row (<W_i, D>)_i
coef_adj W lsum_i l_i W_i, the adjoint of coef_map W

Range duality (Koehler-Sohn Lemma 2.2): for W_1, ..., W_k in 'M[R]_n, if |<l, y>| <= M nuclear (sum_i l_i W_i) for every l, then y = (<W_i, D>)_i for some D with opnorm D <= M, symmetric when the W_i are. By the nearest point of y in the compact convex image of the operator-norm ball, without a general duality theorem.

theorem range_duality main result source, line 284 
forall [R : realType] [k n : nat] [W : 'I_k -> 'M_n] [y : 'rV_k] [M : R],
0 <= M ->
(forall l : 'rV_k, `|mxdot l y| <= M * nuclear (coef_adj W l)) ->
exists2 D : 'M_n, opnorm D <= M & coef_map W D = y

Lemma 2.2 of Koehler and Sohn: if |<l, y>| <= M nuclear (L^* l) for every l, then y = L D for some D with opnorm D <= M.

Main result, as the README states it

Range duality (Koehler-Sohn Lemma 2.2): for W_1, ..., W_k in 'M[R]_n, if |<l, y>| <= M nuclear (sum_i l_i W_i) for every l, then y = (<W_i, D>)_i for some D with opnorm D <= M, symmetric when the W_i are. By the nearest point of y in the compact convex image of the operator-norm ball, without a general duality theorem.
