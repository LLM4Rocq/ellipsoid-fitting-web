GaussianAnalysis/matrix/

matrix/

GaussianAnalysis.matrix17 files7,498 lines410 lemmas and theorems, 47 definitions

The spectral theorem for real symmetric matrices, the positive part of a symmetric matrix and whitening, operator, Frobenius and nuclear norms, nets on spheres and coverings of nuclear-norm balls, von Neumann's trace inequality and the Hoffman-Wielandt inequality, the cone of matrices of bounded condition number, its distance and support function, the maximizer of a trace-penalized quadratic on a closed convex set of matrices, effective ranks, the operator norm of sums of independent random matrices, the largest eigenvalue as the supremum of the quadratic form on the unit sphere, the orthogonal splitting of positive semidefinite matrices.

Guide

Written for this documentation from the sources and the README, and checked against the Rocq statements. The file headers and the statements are the reference.

This directory is the linear algebra of the library: real matrices over an arbitrary realType, with vectors as row or column matrices, the Frobenius inner product mxdot, written <A, B>, and its norm frobenius, written |A|.

The base is the spectral theorem for real symmetric matrices, proved without complex numbers. On it rest operator norms, the largest eigenvalue, sorted spectra, positive parts, nuclear norms, nets, convex sets, coverings and random matrices. Four files serve targets T2 to T5 of applications/.

Reading order

  1. euclidean.v: the Frobenius inner product and norm, orthogonal matrices.
  2. real_spectral.v: Householder reflections and the spectral theorem.
  3. opnorm.v: the operator norm.
  4. whitening.v: whitening a symmetric S >= b I, b > 0.
  5. lambda_max.v: the largest eigenvalue.
  6. spectral_gap.v: a spectral gap from the square, and the Schur test.
  7. nets.v: eps-nets, and operator norms read on nets.
  8. von_neumann.v: the sorted spectrum, von Neumann's and Hoffman-Wielandt's inequalities.
  9. psd_part.v: the positive part of a symmetric matrix.
  10. psd_splitting.v: orthogonal splitting of positive semidefinite matrices.
  11. nuclear.v: the nuclear norm and the range duality.
  12. condition_cone.v: the cone of matrices of bounded condition number.
  13. trace_projection.v: maximizing a trace-penalized quadratic on a closed convex set.
  14. effective_rank.v: few small eigenvalues in a weighted sum of rank-one matrices.
  15. random_opnorm.v: the operator norm of sums of independent random matrices.
  16. covering.v: coverings of nuclear-norm balls by products of column balls.
  17. tensor_packing.v: tensor packing.

Core definitions

  • mxdot, frobenius (euclidean.v): sum_i sum_j A_ij B_ij and sqrt <A, A>.
  • sym_spectralmx, sym_spectral_diag (real_spectral.v): a chosen orthogonal P and row d with A = P^T diag(d) P when A is symmetric.
  • opnorm (opnorm.v): the supremum of |A x| over |x| <= 1.
  • lambda_max (lambda_max.v): the supremum of x^T A x over unit x (0 when n = 0), an eigenvalue for symmetric A when n > 0; frozen with target T3.
  • spec (von_neumann.v): the eigenvalues of a symmetric matrix in decreasing order.
  • psdmx, psd_part (psd_part.v): A symmetric with x^T A x >= 0; the positive part P^T diag((d_i)_+) P.
  • nuclear (nuclear.v): the supremum of <A, B> over opnorm B <= 1.
  • cond_cone, cond_dist (condition_cone.v): the symmetric Q with t I <= Q <= C t I for some t >= 0; the infimum over t >= 0 of sum_i dist(spec H i, [t, C t])^2.
  • trace_quad, trace_argmax (trace_projection.v): 2 <H, S> - |S|^2 - (c / n) (tr S)^2, and its maximizer on K.

Main results

Spectra and norms.

  • symmetric_spectral: every symmetric A is P^T diag(d) P with P orthogonal.
  • opnorm_leP, opnorm_spectral: for b >= 0, opnorm A <= b iff |A x| <= b |x| for all x; for A = P^T diag(d) P with P orthogonal, opnorm A = max_i |d_i|.
  • von_neumann_trace, hoffman_wielandt: for A, B symmetric, tr(A B) <= sum_i spec A i spec B i and sum_i (spec A i - spec B i)^2 <= |A - B|^2.

Duality and convex sets.

  • range_duality: for W_1, ..., W_k in 'M_n and M >= 0, if |<l, y>| <= M nuclear (sum_i l_i W_i) for all l, then y = (<W_i, D>)_i for some D with opnorm D <= M.
  • cond_cone_dist: for C >= 1 and H symmetric, the infimum of |H - Q|^2 over Q in cond_cone C is cond_dist C H.
  • trace_argmaxP, trace_argmax_ge: for c > -1 and K nonempty, convex and closed in the coordinates of mxvec, X = trace_argmax K c H lies in K and trace_quad c H S + min(1, 1 + c) |S - X|^2 <= trace_quad c H X on K.

Nets and random matrices.

  • net_exists: for r >= 0 and eps > 0, every set in the radius-r ball of 'cV_n has an eps-net of at most (2 e (1 + 2 r / eps))^n of its points.
  • opnorm_sum_subexponential: for x of law tuple_prod mu n and m x m matrices Y with forms v^T Y v, |v| <= 1, centered and subexponential with constant L > 0 under each mu k, the symmetric part of sum_k a_k Y(x_k), |a_k| <= 1, has operator norm below a measurable N a with E N a <= 96 L (sqrt (n (m + 1)) + m + 1).
  • product_covering: for c0, gam > 0, an integer d > 0 and m, q in [c0 d, C0 d], a set of m x q matrices A with <A, B> <= gam d opnorm B for all B is covered by at most exp((96 C0 + 33) / c0^2)^(m q) balls of radius gam for the largest column norm.

Targets of applications/.

  • psd_splitting (T5): for L + 1 positive semidefinite A_z, some orthogonal projections P_s of sum 1, with P_s P_t = 0 for s != t, satisfy sum_z sum_(s != z) |P_s A_z P_s|^2 <= 2^L sum_z sum_(z' != z) tr(A_z A_z').
  • tensor_packing (T2): for x_a, y_a in the unit balls with |<x_a, x_b>|, |<y_a, y_b>| <= eta for a != b outside relations Gx, Gy with at most L successors per vertex, Frobenius-orthonormal A_1, ..., A_r with r <= d, d >= 1, 0 < eps <= 1 and 0 <= eta <= eps^2 / 10^4, at most 30000 (1 + L) / eps^2 exp(30000 sqrt d ln(2 + d) / eps^2) indices have sum_k (x_a A_k y_a^T)^2 >= eps^2.
  • psd_square_gap, opnorm_row_sum (T4): for B symmetric positive semidefinite with k x^T B x <= |B x|^2 for all x, k |x|^2 <= x^T B x on the orthogonal of the kernel; for B symmetric, rho >= 0 and every sum_j |B_ij| <= rho, opnorm B <= rho.

How the proofs go

  • symmetric_spectral: the quadratic form is maximal on the compact unit sphere at an eigenvector; a Householder reflection splits off a dimension.
  • von_neumann_trace: the squared entries of an orthogonal matrix form a doubly stochastic matrix; Abel summation concludes without Birkhoff's theorem.
  • range_duality projects y on the image of the operator-norm ball (convex/moreau.v).
  • product_covering rests on Sudakov's minoration and its dual, not on Urysohn's inequality.
  • tensor_packing cuts two spectra at sqrt d, bounds clusters of almost orthogonal vectors, then applies Sudakov's minoration and gauss_mx_series_opnorm_log.

Connections

The first ten files and effective_rank.v need only MathComp, MathComp-Analysis and this directory; the other six draw on gaussian/, convex/moreau.v, probability/, comparison/, fourier/small_ball.v and random_matrix/matrix_series.v.

Every other library directory requires euclidean.v, and random_matrix/ six more files. theories/ and extension/ use the directory at Stdlib's reals; applications/ uses it for targets T1 to T5.

cond_cone C is the set of the symmetric Q with t I <= Q <= C t I for some t >= 0: the positive semidefinite matrices of condition number at most C, and 0. A symmetric Q is in it iff its eigenvalues lie in some [t, C t] with t >= 0; for C >= 0, iff Q is positive semidefinite with |y|^2 x^T Q x <= C |x|^2 y^T Q y for all x, y. In the coordinates of mxvec it is a closed convex cone, as the cone projection of convex/moreau.v asks.

For C >= 1 and H symmetric, the squared Frobenius distance from H to cond_cone C is cond_dist C H, the infimum over t >= 0 of sum_i dist(lambda_i(H), [t, C t])^2: the lower bound by Hoffman-Wielandt, the upper bound by clipping the eigenvalues of H to [t, C t] (Tanaka and Nakata). The support function, the supremum of <H, Q> over the Q of the cone with |Q|_F <= 1, is the norm of the projection of H onto the cone, and its square is |H|_F^2 - cond_dist C H (Moreau's decomposition).
