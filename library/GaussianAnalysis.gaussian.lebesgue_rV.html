lebesgue_rV n is Lebesgue measure on 'rV[R]_n, built as gauss n is: the image of the product of Lebesgue measure on the line (first coordinate) and lebesgue_rV n' (the others) under rV_cons. It is sigma-finite, gives a box the product of the lengths of its sides (lebesgue_rV_box), and it is the only measure that does (lebesgue_rV_unique). Integrals against it are iterated integrals over the first coordinate and the others (ge0_integral_lebesgue_rV_cons), or over two blocks of coordinates (ge0_integral_lebesgue_rV_row_mx); the integral of a product of functions of the coordinates is the product of their integrals (ge0_integral_lebesgue_rV_prod).

It is invariant under translations (lebesgue_rV_shift) and permutations of the coordinates (lebesgue_rV_col_perm), and x |-> a x multiplies it by |a|^-n (lebesgue_rV_scale). The standard Gaussian measure gauss n has the density gauss_pdf with respect to it (ge0_integral_gauss_pdf, gauss_lebesgue_rV). Finally, the integral of exp (- frobenius (x - z) ^ 2 / (2 s^2)) is (sqrt (2 pi) s)^n (lebesgue_rV_gauss_integral), and the closed Euclidean ball of radius r has measure at most (sqrt (2 pi e / n) r)^n (lebesgue_rV_ball_le). Here frobenius x is the Euclidean norm of x (matrix/euclidean.v), and frobenius x ^ 2 = mxdot x x.
