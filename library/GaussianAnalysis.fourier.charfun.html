MathComp-Analysis has no complex numbers, so the characteristic function t |-> E[exp (i t X)] of a law mu on R is given by its real part cf_re mu and its imaginary part cf_im mu. A pair z : R * R stands for the complex number z.1 + i z.2: cmul is the complex product and cmod the modulus.

Laws on the real line are probabilities on measurableTypeR R, the type on which MathComp-Analysis defines Lebesgue measure and normal_prob. A law nu is the image of a probability P on T under a measurable g : T -> R when P (g @^-1` A) = nu A for every measurable A; then cf_re nu t is the integral of cos (t * g x) against P (cf_image). Independent variables are described by product measures (P1 \x P2, tuple_prod).

The characteristic function of normal_prob m s is exp (- s^2 t^2 / 2) (cos (m t), sin (m t)) (cf_normal): for the standard law, differentiation under the integral and Stein's identity (normal_ibp) give the differential equation y' = - t y.
