For a bounded measurable F : 'rV[R]_n -> R whose third derivative along the coordinate j is at most c_j in absolute value, the average of F over the 2^n rows of signs and its integral against gauss n differ by at most (1/2) \sum_j c_j (rademacher_gauss_lindeberg). This is the coordinate-replacement form of the Lindeberg principle (Chatterjee, 'A generalization of the Lindeberg principle', Ann. Probab. 34 (2006), Theorem 1.1) for uniform signs and standard Gaussians, which have the same first two moments.

Part of target T6 of phase 12: a Rocq formalization of the credited argument of the first paragraph of the proof of Theorem ext:sign of 'The free energy of the Ising random perceptron' (family 222 of the OpenAI math collection, revision 1581d4cbd, sections/extensions.tex). The collection has no Lean version of it.

The coordinates are replaced one at a time, by induction on n: Fubini along rV_cons for gauss n.+1 (Rintegral_gauss_cons) and the decomposition of the rows of signs by their first coordinate (sum_sign_rowS). In one coordinate, Taylor's formula of order two and the first two moments give the constant (1 + E |g|^3) / 6 <= 1/2.

Proved:

  • derive_le_cmp: |g b - g a| <= k b - k a when |g'| <= k' on [a, b]; taylor2_le: |f t - f 0 - f'(0) t - f''(0) t^2 / 2| <= c |t|^3 / 6 when |f'''| <= c everywhere.
  • Rintegral_normal_quadratic: E (a + b g + c g^2) = a + c, and Rintegral_normal_abs3: E |g|^3 <= 2, for g of law N(0, 1).
  • rademacher_gauss_step: |(f 1 + f (-1)) / 2 - E f(g)| <= c / 2 when |f'''| <= c everywhere.
  • Rintegral_gauss_cons: for F bounded and measurable on 'rV[R]_n.+1, the integral of F against gauss n.+1 is the integral against gauss n of y |-> \int[N]_t F (rV_cons (t, y)).
  • rademacher_gauss_lindeberg: the comparison above.

rademacher_gauss_lindeberg
Lindeberg's replacement of uniform signs by Gaussians (Chatterjee 2006, Theorem 1.1, for signs and standard Gaussians): if F : 'rV[R]_n -> R is measurable and bounded, and for every x and j the map t |-> F (t e_j + x) has three derivatives with the third at most c_j in absolute value, then the average of F over the 2^n rows of signs and its integral against gauss n differ by at most (1/2) sum_j c_j. By induction on n, with Fubini along rV_cons for bounded measurable functions (Rintegral_gauss_cons) and the decomposition of the rows of signs by their first coordinate (sum_sign_rowS).

Theorem rademacher_gauss_lindeberg n (F : 'rV[R]_n -> R) (C : R)
    (c : 'I_n -> R) :
  measurable_fun setT F -> (forall x, `|F x| <= C) ->
  (forall (j : 'I_n) (x : 'rV[R]_n), exists F1 F2 F3 : R -> R,
    [/\ forall t : R, is_derive t 1 (fun s => F (s *: delta_mx 0 j + x)) (F1 t),
        forall t : R, is_derive t 1 F1 (F2 t),
        forall t : R, is_derive t 1 F2 (F3 t) &
        forall t, `|F3 t| <= c j]) ->
  `|(2 ^+ n)^-1 * \sum_(s : {ffun 'I_n -> bool}) F (sign_row s) -
    \int[gauss n]_x F x| <= 2^-1 * \sum_j c j.
