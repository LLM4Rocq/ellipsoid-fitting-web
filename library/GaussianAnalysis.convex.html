GaussianAnalysis/convex/

convex/

GaussianAnalysis.convex12 files7,573 lines269 lemmas and theorems, 44 definitions

Self-concordant barriers and the log-determinant barrier, minimizers of least squares objectives with a barrier, the Lindeberg replacement for barrier-regularized least squares with regular features and the removal of the barrier and of the ridge, projections onto closed convex sets and Moreau's decomposition for closed convex cones.

Guide

Written for this documentation from the sources and the README, and checked against the Rocq statements. The file headers and the statements are the reference.

This directory holds Theorem 2.9 of Koehler and Sohn, Universality and sharp thresholds for ellipsoid fitting (arXiv 2608.27372): a Lindeberg argument for least squares with a self-concordant barrier. Around it are the convex analysis the theorem needs, the removal of the barrier (Lemma 2.11), and Moreau's decomposition for closed convex cones.

The setting is a subspace V of 'rV[R]_N, a convex set D open in its affine hull z + V, and MathComp's directional derivatives 'D_h along V; matrices enter through mxvec. Self-concordant barriers, such as - ln det on trace slices of the positive definite cone, regularize least squares objectives of n features. For n independent features from two regular laws with the same covariance, the optimal values have close means and concentrate.

Reading order

  1. self_concordant.v: self-concordant functions and barriers, the Dikin ellipsoid, Bregman and barrier bounds.
  2. logdet_barrier.v: - ln det and its two-sided form as barriers on trace slices of the positive definite cone.
  3. least_squares.v: the objectives, their values, and regular features.
  4. minimizers.v: minimizers, first-order conditions, and Taylor's formula with a Bregman remainder.
  5. ls_insertion.v: pointwise bounds on the cost of inserting one feature.
  6. feature_moments.v: moments of linear and quadratic forms under a regular feature.
  7. gauss_feature.v: Gaussian features are regular.
  8. feature_mixture.v: conditions under which a finite mixture of centered laws is a regular feature.
  9. barrier_removal.v: the cost of the barrier in the value.
  10. barrier_lindeberg.v: measurability of the value, a telescoping lemma, and Theorem 2.9.
  11. ridge_comparison.v: concentration of the value without barrier.
  12. moreau.v: projections onto closed convex sets and Moreau's decomposition.

Core definitions

  • rel_open_convex (self_concordant.v): D is convex, y - z is in V for y, z in D, and D is open in z + V.
  • sc_function, sc_barrier (self_concordant.v): B is three times differentiable along V, with a gradient and a Hessian, D^2 B[h,h] >= 0, (D^3 B[h,h,h])^2 <= 4 (D^2 B[h,h])^3, and B tends to +oo along segments leaving D; a theta-barrier also has (D B[h])^2 <= theta D^2 B[h,h].
  • pd_slice, logdet_barrier, pd_slice2, logdet_barrier2 (logdet_barrier.v): the positive definite matrices of trace s with - ln det, and the matrices of trace n strictly between m I and M I with the two-sided barrier.
  • ls_objective (least_squares.v): F_Z(theta, b; beta) = (2n)^-1 sum_i (b - <Z_i, theta>)^2 + beta (lambda |theta|^2 + b^2).
  • ls_barrier_value, ls_value (least_squares.v): Phi_Z(D, B; beta, gamma), the infimum of F_Z(theta, b; beta) + gamma B theta over theta in D and real b, and Phi_Z(D; beta), the same without barrier.
  • regular_feature (least_squares.v): regular_feature K tau Sigma mu means lambda = opnorm Sigma > 0, mu is centered with covariance Sigma, E <z, theta>^4 <= K^4 lambda^2 |theta|^4, and Var (z T z^T) <= tau^2 |T|_F^2 for T symmetric.
  • proj, polar (moreau.v): the nearest point of K to z, when it exists, and the polar cone.

Main results

Throughout, D is convex and relatively open with directions V.

Self-concordance.

  • sc_taylor3 (Lemma 2.15 (ii)): for B self-concordant on D, z in D and h in V, if the local norm r = sqrt (D^2 B(z)[h,h]) (sc_norm) is at most 1/2, then z + h is in D and |B (z + h) - B z - D B(z)[h] - r^2 / 2| <= 2 r^3 / 3.
  • sc_barrier_contraction (Lemma 2.10): for a theta-barrier with theta >= 0, x0, x in D and 0 < eps < 1, B ((1 - eps) x + eps x0) <= B x0 + theta ln (1 / eps).
  • logdet_sc_barrier, logdet2_sc_barrier (Lemma 2.8): with the symmetric matrices of trace 0 as directions, logdet_barrier n is an n-self-concordant barrier on pd_slice n s, and logdet_barrier2 n m M a 2n-self-concordant barrier on pd_slice2 n m M.

Minimizers and features.

  • sc_minimizer: if D is nonempty, B >= 0 is self-concordant on D, gamma > 0, and g is continuous, convex on D and at least kappa |z|^2 with kappa > 0, then g + gamma B attains its minimum on D.
  • ins_cost_quadratic (Proposition 2.14): for B >= 0 self-concordant on D, lambda, beta, gamma > 0, 36 <= n gamma and a minimizer (theta_m, b_m) on D x R of the leave-one-out objective without feature i, the cost Delta(z) of inserting z as feature i (ins_cost) satisfies |Delta(z) - r(z)^2 / (2 (n + l(z)))| <= (2 + 3 r(z)^4) / (n sqrt (n gamma)), where r(z) = b_m - <z, theta_m> and l(z) = <alpha(z), H^-1 alpha(z)> (ins_ell), H the Hessian at the minimizer.
  • gauss_regular_feature: for S != 0, the law of x S with x of law gauss k is (3^(1/4), sqrt 2 opnorm (S^T S))-regular with covariance S^T S.
  • mixture_regular_feature: a finite mixture, with weights w_i, of centered laws with second-moment matrices Sig_i, E_i <z, theta>^4 <= A |theta|^4 and Var_i (z T z^T) <= tau^2 |T|_F^2 for T symmetric is (K, sqrt (tau^2 + c^2))-regular with covariance Sigma = sum_i w_i Sig_i, if opnorm Sigma > 0, sum_i w_i |Sig_i - Sigma|_F^2 <= c^2 and A <= K^4 opnorm Sigma^2.

Universality of the values (Theorem 2.9). Let mu and nu be (K, tau)-regular with the same covariance Sigma, lambda = opnorm Sigma, tau >= 0, D nonempty, B >= 0 self-concordant on D, n > 0, beta, gamma > 0, Phi = Phi_Z(D, B; beta, gamma), and Lref = (1 + 1/beta) (1 + inf over D of lambda |theta|^2 + gamma B theta) (ls_ref_scale).

  • barrier_lindeberg_mean: if 36 <= n gamma, then |E_(mu^n) Phi - E_(nu^n) Phi| <= 100 (1 + K^4)^2 Lref^2 ((n gamma)^(-1/2) + sqrt (dim V) (tau / lambda + 1) / n).
  • barrier_lindeberg_variance, barrier_lindeberg_concentration: Var_(mu^n) Phi <= 4 (1 + K^4)^2 Lref^2 / n; for t > 0 and 36 <= n gamma, |Phi - E_(nu^n) Phi| exceeds the mean bound plus t with mu^n-probability at most 4 (1 + K^4)^2 Lref^2 / (n t^2).

Removing the barrier.

  • barrier_removal (Lemma 2.11): for every tuple Z, a theta-barrier B >= 0 on D with theta >= 0, theta_ref in D, b_ref real, lambda, beta >= 0, gamma > 0 and 0 < eps < 1, 0 <= Phi_Z(D, B; beta, gamma) - Phi_Z(D; beta) <= eps F_Z(theta_ref, b_ref; beta) + gamma B theta_ref + gamma theta ln (1 / eps).
  • ridge_comparison: under the hypotheses of Theorem 2.9, with B a theta-barrier, theta >= 0, theta_0 in D, 0 < eps < 1, t, M > 0 and 36 <= n gamma, |Phi_Z(D; beta) - E_(nu^n) Phi_Z(D, B; beta, gamma)| exceeds the mean bound plus t + eps M + gamma B theta_0 + gamma theta ln (1 / eps) with mu^n-probability at most 4 (1 + K^4)^2 Lref^2 / (n t^2) + (theta_0 Sigma theta_0^T / 2 + beta lambda |theta_0|^2) / M.

Projections.

  • proj_variational, proj_nonexpansive: for K closed, convex and nonempty, p in K is nearest to z iff <z - p, y - p> <= 0 for y in K, and proj K is 1-Lipschitz.
  • moreau (Lemma 5.1), cone_proj_support: for a closed convex cone C, z = proj C z + proj (polar C) z with orthogonal parts, and the supremum of <z, y> over y in C with |y| <= 1 is |proj C z|.

How the proofs go

  • Bounds on the Hessian along a segment, from a comparison in one variable (sc_ode_ge, sc_ode_le), give the Bregman bounds of Lemma 2.15 and, with the blow-up of B at the exit point, the Dikin ellipsoid (sc_dikin).
  • logdet_line writes - ln det (A + t U) as c - sum_i ln (1 + t l_i) by simultaneous diagonalization, and logsum_sc_barrier checks the barrier conditions in one variable.
  • Theorem 2.9 follows the paper: measurability through a countable dense subset of D (measurable_inf_rel_open), a telescoping over the coordinates (lindeberg_telescope) with loo_compare at each step, built on insertion_compare, Efron-Stein for the variance and Chebyshev for the concentration. The paper asks D to be bounded and B to be a barrier; the proof uses neither: the ridge term makes the objective coercive, and a self-concordant B suffices.
  • Lemma 2.11 contracts toward the reference point; ridge_comparison adds Markov's inequality for F_Z(theta_0, 0; beta); mixtures use the law of total variance.

moreau.v
proj_existsproj_variationalprojPproj_uniqueproj_nonexpansive
Projections in 'rV[R]_N with the Frobenius inner product: a nonempty closed set has a nearest point to every z (compactness of the closed bounded sets); for K closed, convex and nonempty, p in K is nearest to z iff <z - p, y - p> <= 0 for every y in K, the nearest point proj K z is unique, and |proj K z - proj K z'| <= |z - z'|.
moreaumoreau_normcone_projPcone_proj_supportpolar
Lemma 5.1 of Koehler and Sohn (Moreau's decomposition): for a closed convex cone C, z = proj C z + proj (polar C) z with the two parts orthogonal, so |z|^2 = |proj C z|^2 + |proj (polar C) z|^2; p in C is proj C z iff z - p is in the polar cone and orthogonal to p; and sup_(y in C, |y| <= 1) <z, y> = |proj C z|.

Under a law on 'rV[R]_N with E <z, theta>^4 <= A |theta|^4 for every theta, a measurable g with |g z| <= a + b |z|^4 for every z (a bound on the absolute value) is integrable, so the linear forms, their products and the quadratic forms z T z^T are integrable and z T z^T is square integrable; if moreover E <z, theta> <z, theta'> = theta Sig theta'^T, then E z T z^T = <Sig^T, T>.
mixture_regular_feature_dot
A finite mixture of laws P i on 'rV[R]_N, each centered with second-moment matrix Sig i, E_i <z, theta>^4 <= A |theta|^4 and Var_i (z T z^T) <= tau^2 |T|_F^2 for T symmetric, is a regular feature with covariance Sigma = sum_i w_i Sig i, any K with A <= K^4 opnorm Sigma^2, and sqrt (tau^2 + c^2) in place of tau, when opnorm Sigma > 0 and sum_i w_i <Sig i - Sigma, T>^2 <= c^2 |T|_F^2 for every symmetric T (the law of total variance, with E_i z T z^T - E z T z^T = <Sig i - Sigma, T> for T symmetric).
mixture_regular_feature
The same when opnorm Sigma > 0 and sum_i w_i |Sig i - Sigma|_F^2 <= c^2, which implies the hypothesis of mixture_regular_feature_dot by Cauchy-Schwarz for the Frobenius product; no structure of Sig i - Sigma is needed.
