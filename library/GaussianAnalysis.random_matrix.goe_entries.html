For a of law gauss (d * d) and the GOE matrix W = goe_mx a of random_matrix/goe.v, each entry of W is a linear form of a: W_ij = <a, goe_coef i j> (goe_mx_mxdot). The coefficient vector of a diagonal entry has squared norm 2 / d (frobenius_goe_coef_diag), and the coefficient vectors of the entries of a row outside the diagonal are orthogonal, of squared norm 1 / d (mxdot_goe_coef_row). Hence, for s >= 0 and u > 0,

P(|W_ii| >= s) <= 2 exp (- d s^2 / 4)               (goe_mx_diag_tail),
P(|W_ij| >= s) <= 2 exp (- d s^2 / 2) if i != j  (goe_mx_offdiag_tail),
P(sum_(j != i) W_ij^2 >= 1 + u) <= exp (- d u^2 / (4 (1 + u))),

the last one (goe_mx_row_tail) because d sum_(j != i) W_ij^2 has the law of a chi-square with d - 1 degrees of freedom (gauss_mulmx_law, then gauss_centered_chi2_upper_tail of comparison/chi_square.v). Union bounds over the d diagonal entries, the d^2 entries and the d rows give the tails of the maxima, with the factors 2 d, 2 d^2 and d (goe_mx_diag_max_tail, goe_mx_offdiag_max_tail, goe_mx_row_max_tail); their events are measurable (measurable_goe_diag_max and the next two).

Three general facts are proved on the way: removing the diagonal of a square matrix A costs at most max_i |A_ii| in operator norm (opnorm_sub_diag_le, from opnorm_diag_mx_le); the union bound mu (\bigcup_i F i) <= \sum_i mu (F i) for a measure mu, a finite index type and measurable sets F i (measure_bigcup_fin_le); and x^k exp (- x c) <= 6 / (c^3 x) for c > 0, x >= 1 and k <= 2 (exprn_expRN_le), so that such tails, times at most d^2 events, are of order 1 / d.

Part of target T4 of phase 12: a Rocq formalization of the credited argument of the proof of the proposition 'Good disorder' (rcut:prop:good-disorder) of 'Cutoff throughout the high-temperature Sherrington-Kirkpatrick phase' (family 227 of the OpenAI math collection, revision 1581d4cbd, file appendices/entropy.tex), for the diagonal entries, the off-diagonal entries and the row sums of squares of the GOE matrix; the proposition itself belongs to target T4. It follows the TeX, not a Lean module of the collection. The manuscript bounds a row sum by the exponential Markov bound with E exp (theta (g^2 - 1)) <= exp (C theta^2) for |theta| <= 1/4; the chi-square tail used here has the same form with explicit constants.
