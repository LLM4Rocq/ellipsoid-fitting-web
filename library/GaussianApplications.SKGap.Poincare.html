Part of target T4 of phase 12 (AGENTS.md, section Phase 12). A Rocq formalization of the credited argument of the proposition 'Gap and covariance under scaled interactions and fields' of 'Cutoff throughout the high-temperature Sherrington-Kirkpatrick phase' (family 227 of the OpenAI math collection, revision 1581d4cbd, appendices/entropy.tex). The Lean counterpart:

Lean: exists_uniform_gap_tolerance (SKRatio/Variational/UniformGap.lean)

  • ising_gen_sq_ge, the sum of the two-spin inequality (ising_two_spin of TwoSpin.v) over the ordered pairs: with T = (tanh A_ij), T2 = (tanh^2 A_ij), opnorm T <= M, 2 opnorm T2 <= M and sum_j |tanh A_ij|^3 <= rho for every i, and |tanh A_ij| <= 1/1000: nu[(sum_i D_i f)^2] >= (1 - M - 140000 rho) E(f). The two quadratic forms are bounded by operator norms, with v_i^2 (1 + m_i^2) <= v_i, as in the manuscript.
  • ising_cov_from_gap: from k Var_nu(f) <= E(f) for every f, opnorm Cov_nu(x) <= 1 / k, by the linear functions f(x) = <c, x>, for which d_i f = c_i (half_diff_form).
  • sk_gap_deterministic: for 0 <= beta < 1/2, 0 < delta <= sk_gap_tol beta = min (1/2000) ((1 - 2 beta) / 400000), J symmetric with zero diagonal, opnorm J <= 2 beta + delta, sum_j J_ij^2 <= beta^2 + delta for every row, |J_ij| <= delta, and 0 <= s <= 1: kappa Var_nu(f) <= E(f) for every f and opnorm Cov_nu(x) <= 1 / kappa, with kappa = (1 - 2 beta) / 2 and nu the Ising law of s J with any field. It combines ising_gen_sq_ge, the bounds |tanh u| <= |u| and |tanh u - u| <= |u|^3 with the Schur test (opnorm_row_sum of library/matrix/spectral_gap.v), and the spectral step ising_gap_from_square of Spectral.v. The manuscript states the gap for all large n on its good-disorder event; here the tolerance is explicit and works for every n.
  • sk_coupling_sym, sk_coupling_diag: the SK coupling is symmetric with zero diagonal.
