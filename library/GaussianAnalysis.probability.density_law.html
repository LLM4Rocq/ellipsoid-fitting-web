For a probability mu on a measurable type T and a measurable f : T -> R, nonnegative everywhere, of integral one against mu, density_prob mu f is the probability A |-> int_A f dmu. Its measure is the integral of a nonnegative function, sigma-additive by semi_sigma_additive_nng_induced of MathComp-Analysis. When f is not such a density, density_prob mu f is mu itself (density_prob_default), so that density_prob mu f is a probability for every f; the results below assume prob_density mu f. density_law mu f hf is the same probability, built from a proof hf of prob_density mu f: its value on A is int_A f dmu by definition, with no fallback.

  • density_probE: density_prob mu f A = int_A f dmu.
  • density_prob_dominates: density_prob mu f is absolutely continuous with respect to mu.
  • ge0_integral_density_prob, integrable_density_prob, integral_density_prob: integrating g against density_prob mu f is integrating g f against mu, for g nonnegative or integrable.
  • density_prob_ae, eq_density_prob: a density may be changed on a mu-null set: int_A f' dmu = density_prob mu f A for measurable A when f' = f mu-almost everywhere, even if f' is negative somewhere, and two densities equal mu-almost everywhere give the same probability.
  • prob_density_tuple_prod, tuple_prod_density: if f_k is a density against nu_k for every k < n, the product of the laws density_prob nu_k f_k, k < n, has the density x |-> prod_k f_k (x_k) against tuple_prod nu n. indic_tuple_box: the indicator of a box is the product of the indicators of its sides.
  • density_lawE: density_law mu f hf A = int_A f dmu. density_law_prob: density_law mu f hf is density_prob mu f. integral_density_law_prob: integrals against the two agree, so every result on density_prob holds for density_law. density_law_dominates, ge0_integral_density_law, integrable_density_law, integral_density_law, eq_density_law and tuple_prod_density_law restate the results above for density_law.
