Continuity of a parameter integral

cvg_seqP is the sequential criterion for the limit of a function at a point of a pseudometric space: f x tends to l as x tends to x0 as soon as f (u n) tends to l for every sequence u that tends to x0.

continuity_under_integral_at: if f x y is, for every y in B, continuous in x at x0, and `|f x y| <= g y for x in a neighbourhood of x0 and y in B, with g integrable on B, then x |-> \int[mu]_(y in B) f x y is continuous at x0, by dominated convergence along sequences. MathComp-Analysis states this for a real parameter in an open interval (continuity_under_integral); here the parameter ranges over a pseudometric space, such as 'rV[R]_n.
