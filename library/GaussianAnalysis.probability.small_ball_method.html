Mendelson's small-ball method, in the form of Koltchinskii and Mendelson (2015, Theorem 2.1), cited by Koehler and Sohn (arXiv 2608.27372, lines 4764-4779): a uniform lower bound for the empirical second moments of a class of functions, from a lower bound on its small-ball probabilities and an upper bound on its Rademacher complexity. The deviation of the empirical process is controlled in expectation, by symmetrization and contraction (symmetrization.v), and then by Markov's inequality, so the probability of failure is of the order of the complexity: this suffices for limits in probability when the scale grows, and no bounded differences inequality is needed.

Main results, for x of law tuple_prod mu n with n > 0:

  • small_ball_method: for a sequence of measurable f_j, u > 0 and q > 0 with mu_k(|f_j| >= 2 u) >= q for all j and k < n, and r with
    E rademacher_mean (max_(j < J) |(1/n) sum_k eps_k f_j(x_k)|) <= r

    for every J, the event small_ball_event n f (n u^2 q / 2), that some f_j has sum_k f_j(x_k)^2 < n u^2 q / 2, has probability at most 8 r / (u q). It is measurable (measurable_small_ball_event). Proof: the ramp between u and 2 u, which is 1/u-Lipschitz (ramp_lipschitz), symmetrization and contraction for the initial segment j < J, Markov's inequality, and the continuity of measures along the union over J.

  • paley_zygmund_half: if E X^2 = 1 and E X^4 <= K, then P(|X| >= 1/2) >= 9 / (16 K) (paley_zygmund of moments.v for X^2).
  • dense_seq_rV: every nonempty set D of row vectors contains a sequence that is dense in D, from rational approximations (rat_approx_rV).
  • small_ball_linear: let F : T -> 'rV[R]_N, S a square matrix, K > 0 and D a set of row vectors such that, for p in D and k < n, E_k <F, p>^2 = p S p^T > 0 and E_k <F, p>^4 <= K (p S p^T)^2, where <., .> is mxdot. If Psi is measurable in x, with
    |(1/n) sum_k eps_k <F(x_k), p>| <= sqrt (p S p^T) Psi(x, eps)

    for p in D, and E rademacher_mean (Psi x) <= r, then there is a measurable event of probability at most 512 K r / 9 outside of which

    (1/n) sum_k <F(x_k), p>^2 >= 9 / (512 K) p S p^T

    for every p in D. Proof: small_ball_method for the normalized class on a dense sequence of D, with u = 1/4 and q = 9 / (16 K) from paley_zygmund_half, and the continuity of quadratic forms (mxdot_form_lipschitz, mxdot_form_outer). Removing the ridge (Lemma 2.12) uses it with the features augmented by a constant coordinate for the bias.

  • Moments of affine images, used for the affine forms of Lemma 2.12: integrable_pow4 (a finite fourth moment makes X and X^2 integrable), integral_affine_sqr, E (X - b)^2 = E X^2 - 2 b E X + b^2, integral_affine_pow4_le, E (X - b)^4 <= 8 (E X^4 + b^4), and markov_ge0, P(Y > B) <= E Y / B for Y >= 0 and B > 0.
