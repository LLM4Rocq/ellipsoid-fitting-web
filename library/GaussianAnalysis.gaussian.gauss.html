gauss n is the law of a row vector of n independent standard normal coordinates: the n-fold product of normal_prob 0 1 on 'rV[R]_n, with the sigma-algebra of measurable_matrix.v. It is defined by recursion on n, the first coordinate being drawn first (gaussS). The measure of a box is the product of the measures of its sides (gauss_box), and this property characterizes gauss n among all measures (gauss_unique). Measurable functions of polynomial growth are integrable (integrable_gauss_poly).

The vectors are rows because the linear algebra of MathComp writes them so, with linear maps acting on the right: if x has law gauss n and A is an n x m matrix, then x *m A is a centered Gaussian vector with covariance A^T *m A.

rV_cons pthe row vector of size n.+1 with first coordinate p.1, and then the coordinates of p.2 : 'rV_n
gauss nthe standard Gaussian measure on 'rV[R]_n
