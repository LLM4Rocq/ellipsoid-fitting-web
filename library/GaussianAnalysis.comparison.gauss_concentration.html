Let x have law gauss n and let F : 'rV[R]_n -> R be L-Lipschitz for the Euclidean norm: |F x - F y| <= L |x - y|. Then E[expR (t (F x - E[F]))] <= expR (t^2 L^2 / 2) for every real t (gauss_concentration), with the sharp constant of Tsirelson, Ibragimov and Sudakov. For u >= 0, P(F x >= E[F] + u) and P(F x <= E[F] - u) are at most expR (- u^2 / (2 L^2)) (gauss_concentration_upper_tail, gauss_concentration_lower_tail), and P(|F x - E[F]| >= u) is at most twice that (gauss_concentration_abs_tail). The variance of F x is at most L^2: this is the Gaussian Poincare inequality for Lipschitz functions (gauss_poincare_lipschitz), from the bound on E[expR (t (F x - E[F]))] at t and - t, with cosh y >= 1 + y^2 / 2 (expR_cosh_ge), as t -> 0.

The proof follows Herbst. Let F be bounded, of class C^1, with gradient of Euclidean norm at most L, and let t >= 0. Let y be an independent copy of x and phi th = E[F x expR (t F (cos th x + sin th y))]. After the rotation of (x, y) by th, Gaussian integration by parts in y (gauss2_ibp_snd) shows that the derivative of phi is at least - sin th t L^2 E[expR (t F)]. Comparing phi 0 and phi (pi / 2) gives the covariance inequality E[F expR (t F)] <= (E[F] + t L^2) E[expR (t F)] (gauss_herbst_ineq), and integrating the resulting differential inequality for t |-> E[expR (t F)] gives the bound for such F (gauss_concentration_smooth). A general Lipschitz F is the pointwise limit of Gaussian smoothings (gauss_smoothing.v) of F clipped to [-(k + 1), k + 1] (clip), and Fatou's lemma concludes.

The file also proves facts on the law gauss n \x gauss n of two independent standard Gaussian vectors: its marginals (integral_gauss2_fst, integral_gauss2_snd), the independence of its components (integral_gauss2_prod) and integration by parts in the second component (gauss2_ibp_snd).
