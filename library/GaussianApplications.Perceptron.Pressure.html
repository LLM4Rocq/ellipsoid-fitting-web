Part of target T6 of phase 12 (AGENTS.md, section Phase 12), the comparison of the expected pressure of the Ising perceptron under sign and Gaussian patterns. A Rocq formalization of the credited argument of the first paragraph of the proof of Theorem ext:sign of 'The free energy of the Ising random perceptron' (family 222 of the OpenAI math collection, revision 1581d4cbd, sections/extensions.tex). The collection has no Lean version of it.

Take patterns G in 'M[R]_(M, N) with N >= 1, and a potential f with three derivatives such that |f| <= B0, |f'| <= B1, |f''| <= B2 and |f'''| <= B3. The normalized pressure p_N(G, f) of PerceptronSignDefinitions.v is (1/N) ln (2^(-N) sum_x exp (E_G(x))), with the energy E_G(x) = sum_mu f ((G x)_mu / sqrt N) (perceptron_pressureE). It is at most M B0 in absolute value (perceptron_pressure_le) and measurable in the entries of G (measurable_perceptron_pressure). As in the manuscript, the entry G_(mu, i) enters only (G x)_mu, with the factor x_i / sqrt N. So the pressure has three derivatives in each entry, and the third is at most (B3 + 6 B1 B2 + 6 B1^3) / (N^2 sqrt N) in absolute value (perceptron_pressure_derive3, from log_partition_derive3 of library/probability/log_partition.v). The coordinate replacement of library/probability/rademacher_lindeberg.v over the M N entries then gives perceptron_sign_compare: g |-> p_N(vec_mx g, f) is integrable against gauss (M * N), and

|E_sign p_N - E_Gauss p_N| <= (B3 + 6 B1 B2 + 6 B1^3) / 2 M N^(-3/2).

The average over the sign patterns indexed by 'I_M * 'I_N is the average over the rows of signs indexed by 'I_(M * N) (sign_pressureE).

The manuscript takes f in C_c^infinity and states the error O_f(N^(-1/2)) when M is of order N. Here the constant is explicit, depends only on B1, B2 and B3, and holds for every M.

pressure_energy G xsum_mu f ((G x)_mu / sqrt N), the energy of the spins x for the patterns G
