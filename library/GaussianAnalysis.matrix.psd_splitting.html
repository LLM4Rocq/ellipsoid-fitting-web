A Rocq formalization of the credited argument of the lemma 'Orthogonal splitting' of 'Exponential PSD rank of positively shifted matching matrices' (family 126 of the OpenAI math collection, revision 1581d4cbd, source/sections/splitting.tex). The collection has no Lean version of it.

For positive semidefinite A_1, ..., A_L of 'M[R]_n, L >= 1, there are orthogonal projections P_1, ..., P_L, possibly zero, mutually orthogonal and of sum 1, such that the sum over z and s != z of |P_s A_z P_s|_F^2 is at most 2^(L-1) times the sum of tr (A_z A_z') over the ordered pairs of distinct indices.

The proof is the manuscript's. The two-matrix estimate: for A, B >= 0, the spectral projection P of A - B onto its nonnegative eigenspaces satisfies tr (A B) >= |P B P|_F^2 + |(1 - P) A (1 - P)|_F^2, because the off-diagonal blocks of A and B along P agree and the diagonal blocks are ordered. Then an induction on L, which splits A_1 from the sum of the others and applies the induction hypothesis to the compressions on the range of 1 - P. To keep the dimension fixed, the induction is relative to an orthogonal projection Q (psd_splitting_rel): the projections are below Q, they sum to Q, and the overlaps are tr (A_z Q A_z' Q). The squared triangle inequality on Q - P uses the nonnegative quadratic form X |-> tr (A X B X), and the spectral projection is built from symmetric_spectral and commutes with every matrix that commutes with A - B (diag_mx_commute_map), hence with Q.

Proved:

  • orthoprojmx1, orthoprojmx0, orthoprojmxB; psdmx_conj, psdmxD, psdmx_sum; mxtrace_psd_ge0 (tr (S T) >= 0 for S, T >= 0); frobenius_sqr_sym.
  • spectral_nonneg_proj: the spectral projection E of a symmetric M onto its nonnegative eigenspaces, with E M = M E, E M E >= 0, (1 - E) M (1 - E) <= 0, and X E = E X for every X that commutes with M.
  • mxdot_block0, mxdot_sym_tr, mxdot_compress: blocks along orthogonal projections; two_matrix_split: the two-matrix estimate, relative to Q.
  • frobenius_compress_le: compressing by projections of sum Q does not increase the Frobenius norm of a matrix supported by Q; frobenius_sum_psd_le: the squared norms of positive semidefinite matrices sum to at most the squared norm of their sum.
  • sum_offdiag_recl: a sum over the ordered pairs of distinct indices of 'I_L.+2 split at the first index; trace_quad_ge0, trace_quad_sub_le: tr (A (Q - P) B (Q - P)) <= 2 tr (A Q B Q) + 2 tr (A P B P).
  • psd_splitting_rel and psd_splitting: the lemma for L + 1 matrices, with constant 2^L.

The lemma 'Orthogonal splitting': for L + 1 positive semidefinite matrices A_z, orthogonal projections P_s, possibly zero, that are mutually orthogonal and sum to the identity, with the sum over z and s != z of |P_s A_z P_s|_F^2 at most 2^L times the sum over the ordered pairs z != z' of tr (A_z A_z').
