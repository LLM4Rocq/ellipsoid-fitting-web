Self-concordant functions and barriers in the sense of Nesterov and Nemirovskii (Interior-point polynomial algorithms, Definition 2.3.1), on a convex set D of 'rV[R]_N that is open in its affine hull z + V, with V a subspace. Derivatives are taken along the directions h of V, with MathComp's directional derivative 'D_h. A self-concordant function is convex, three times differentiable along V, its first and second derivatives are a linear and a quadratic form, it tends to +oo at the relative boundary of D, and

(SC)  |D^3 B(z)[h, h, h]| <= 2 (D^2 B(z)[h, h])^(3/2),

written (D^3 B(z)[h, h, h])^2 <= 4 (D^2 B(z)[h, h])^3. A theta-self-concordant barrier satisfies moreover

(BP)  (D B(z)[h])^2 <= theta D^2 B(z)[h, h].

These are the notions of Koehler and Sohn, Universality and sharp thresholds for ellipsoid fitting (arXiv 2608.27372), Definition 2.7.

rel_open_convex V DD is convex, y - z is in V for y, z in D, and around each z of D, D contains the points z + h with h in V and `|h| < e for some e > 0: D is relatively open in its affine hull z + V
sc_function V D BB is a self-concordant function on D with directions V: three times differentiable along V, with a gradient vector and a Hessian matrix, convex, (SC), and B (z + t h) tends to +oo when t tends to 1 from the left, if z + t h is in D for 0 <= t < 1 and z + h is not
sc_barrier V D theta BB is a theta-self-concordant barrier: a self-concordant function with (BP)
sc_norm B z hthe local norm sqrt (D^2 B(z)[h, h])
bregman_div B y zthe Bregman divergence B y - B z - D B(z)[y - z]
sc_omega rr - ln (1 + r)
sc_omega_star r- r - ln (1 - r)

Main results, for D relatively open and convex with directions V, B a self-concordant function on D, z in D, h in V and r = sc_norm B z h:

  • sc_hessian_line_ge, sc_hessian_line_le: for t >= 0 with z + t h in D, r^2 / (1 + t r)^2 <= D^2 B(z + t h)[h, h], and D^2 B(z + t h)[h, h] is at most r^2 / (1 - t r)^2 when moreover t r < 1 (Koehler-Sohn, proof of Lemma 2.15); the one-dimensional comparison is sc_ode_ge, sc_ode_le.
  • sc_dikin: the Dikin ellipsoid: if r < 1, then z + h is in D (Nesterov-Nemirovskii, Theorem 2.1.1).
  • sc_bregman_ge, sc_bregman_le: sc_omega r <= bregman_div B (z + h) z when z + h is in D, and bregman_div B (z + h) z <= sc_omega_star r when r < 1.
  • sc_bregman_small (Lemma 2.15 (i)): if z + h is in D and bregman_div B (z + h) z <= sc_omega (1/2), then r <= 1/2 and r^2 / 3 <= bregman_div B (z + h) z.
  • sc_taylor3 (Lemma 2.15 (ii)): if r <= 1/2, then z + h is in D and |B (z + h) - B z - D B(z)[h] - r^2 / 2| <= 2 r^3 / 3.
  • sc_function_convex, sc_function_continuous: B is convex on D, and continuous at every point of D for the points of D.
  • sc_barrier_grad_le (Lemma 2.10, Nesterov Theorem 4.2.4): for a theta-self-concordant barrier with theta >= 0 and y, z in D, D B(z)[y - z] <= theta.
  • sc_barrier_contraction (Lemma 2.10): for x0, x in D and 0 < eps < 1, B ((1 - eps) x + eps x0) <= B x0 + theta ln (1 / eps).
  • line_derive3: if B agrees on the line through z along h with a function of one variable with three derivatives, these are the derivatives of B along h at the points of the line in D; with lin_form_vec and bilin_form_mx (a linear form is mxdot g h and a bilinear form is h M h^T), this reduces the conditions of sc_function to facts in one variable (convex/logdet_barrier.v).

Definition sc_function V D B :=
  [/\ forall z h, D z -> h \in V ->
        [/\ derivable B z h, derivable ('D_h B) z h &
            derivable ('D_h ('D_h B)) z h],
      forall z, D z -> exists (g : 'rV[R]_N) (M : 'M[R]_N),
        forall h, h \in V ->
          'D_h B z = mxdot g h /\ 'D_h ('D_h B) z = mxdot h (h *m M),
      forall z h, D z -> h \in V -> 0 <= 'D_h ('D_h B) z,
      forall z h, D z -> h \in V ->
        ('D_h ('D_h ('D_h B)) z) ^+ 2 <= 4%:R * ('D_h ('D_h B) z) ^+ 3 &
      forall z h, D z -> h \in V ->
        (forall t, 0 <= t < 1 -> D (z + t *: h)) -> ~ D (z + h) ->
        B (z + t *: h) @[t --> (1 : R)^'-] --> +oo].
