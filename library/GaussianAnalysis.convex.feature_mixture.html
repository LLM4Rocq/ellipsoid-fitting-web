  • extension/Features/Mixture.v

A finite mixture (probability/mixture.v) of laws P i on 'rV[R]_N, each centered with second-moment matrix Sig i, with E_i <z, theta>^4 <= A |theta|^4 and Var_i (z T z^T) <= tau^2 |T|_F^2 for T symmetric, is a regular feature (regular_feature of least_squares.v) with covariance Sigma = sum_i w_i Sig i as soon as opnorm Sigma > 0. The law of total variance adds to tau^2 the spread c^2 of the covariances: for T symmetric, E_i z T z^T - E z T z^T = <Sig i - Sigma, T>, so it suffices that sum_i w_i <Sig i - Sigma, T>^2 <= c^2 |T|_F^2 for every symmetric T. By Cauchy-Schwarz for the Frobenius product, c^2 >= sum_i w_i |Sig i - Sigma|_F^2 is enough. No structure of Sig i - Sigma is needed.

  • moment4_integrable and its corollaries moment4_int_lin, moment4_int_lin2, moment4_int_quad, moment4_Lfun2_quad: under a law with E <z, theta>^4 <= A |theta|^4 for every theta, a measurable g with |g z| <= a + b |z|^4 is integrable, so the polynomials of degree at most 4 in the coordinates are (as reg_integrable of feature_moments.v for a regular feature);
  • quad_expectation: E z T z^T = <Sig^T, T> for a law with second-moment matrix Sig, that is E <z, theta> <z, theta'> = theta Sig theta'^T;
  • mixture_regular_feature_dot: the mixture is a regular feature with covariance Sigma, any K with A <= K^4 opnorm Sigma^2, and sqrt (tau^2 + c^2) in place of tau, when sum_i w_i <Sig i - Sigma, T>^2 <= c^2 |T|_F^2 for every symmetric T;
  • mixture_regular_feature: the same when sum_i w_i |Sig i - Sigma|_F^2 <= c^2.
