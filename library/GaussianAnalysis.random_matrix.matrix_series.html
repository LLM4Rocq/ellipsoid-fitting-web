For matrices H_1, ..., H_r in 'M[R]_n and g of law gauss r, the Gaussian matrix series X = \sum_j g_j H_j (written \sum_j g 0 j *: H j) has trace moments bounded by its variance S = \sum_j H_j^2 alone: no dimension of the matrices enters the bounds. Through the symmetric dilation of rectangular matrices, the same holds for the operator norm of \sum_j g_j B_j.

This file is a Rocq formalization of the credited argument of Lemma 4.2, 'A Gaussian matrix estimate', of 'Pointwise convergence of fourfold ergodic averages for mixing transformations' (OpenAI, 4 October 2026), family 154 of the OpenAI math collection at revision 1581d4cbd. The same lemma is in the section 'Tensor packing' of its companion 'Pointwise Multiple Ergodic Averages for Mixing Transformations'. There is no Lean version. The formalization differs from the manuscript in four points:

  • the recursion holds for every q, and the manuscript states it for q >= 2;
  • the trace inequality uses x^a y^b + y^a x^b <= x^(a+b) + y^(a+b) for x, y >= 0, where the manuscript uses the weighted arithmetic-geometric mean;
  • the hypotheses opnorm S <= sqrt d and tr S <= 2 d on the dilation S are stated on its two diagonal blocks, \sum_j B_j B_j^T and \sum_j B_j^T B_j;
  • the constant of the last bound is explicit, 5, with q = floor (ln (2 + d)) + 1, where the manuscript takes q >= 2 comparable to log (2 + d).

Proved:

  • mxtrace_sandwich_le: |tr (K A^a K A^b)| <= tr (K^2 A^(a+b)) for K, A symmetric and a + b even, in an eigenbasis of A; mxtrace_mul_psd_le: tr (S T) <= opnorm S tr T for T positive semidefinite.
  • moment_root_le, moment_root_powR: E f <= (E f^(k+1))^(1/(k+1)) for a measurable f >= 0 and a probability, the integrals in the extended reals, from Young's inequality with integer exponents (young_exprn).
  • gauss_mx_series_ibp: E tr X^(k+1) = \sum_j E tr (H_j D_j), where D_j = mxpow_deriv X H_j k is the derivative of X^k along H_j, by Gaussian integration by parts in each coordinate (gauss_ibp_polyfun, is_derive_mxpow); gauss_mx_series_trace_sqr: E tr X^2 = tr S.
  • gauss_mx_series_trace_rec: for H_j symmetric, E tr X^(2q+2) <= (2q + 1) opnorm S E tr X^(2q).
  • gauss_mx_series_moment: for H_j symmetric and q >= 1, E tr X^(2q) <= (2q - 1)!! opnorm S^(q-1) tr S, with (2q - 1)!! = \prod_(i < q) (2i + 1), the integral in the extended reals.
  • sym_dilation B is symmetric, linear in B, with square block_mx (B B^T) 0 0 (B^T B), and opnorm B <= opnorm (sym_dilation B); opnorm_block_diag_le: the operator norm of block_mx A 0 0 D is at most the larger of opnorm A and opnorm D.
  • gauss_mx_series_opnorm_moment, gauss_mx_series_opnorm: for B_j in 'M[R]_(m, p) and q >= 1, E opnorm (\sum_j g_j B_j)^(2q) <= (2q - 1)!! sigma^(2(q-1)) tau, hence E opnorm (\sum_j g_j B_j) <= ((2q - 1)!! sigma^(2(q-1)) tau)^(1/(2q)), where sigma^2 = max (opnorm (\sum_j B_j B_j^T), opnorm (\sum_j B_j^T B_j)) and tau = 2 tr (\sum_j B_j B_j^T).
  • gauss_mx_series_opnorm_log: if opnorm (\sum_j B_j B_j^T) and opnorm (\sum_j B_j^T B_j) are at most sqrt d and tr (\sum_j B_j B_j^T) <= d for a real d >= 1, then E opnorm (\sum_j g_j B_j) <= 5 d^(1/4) sqrt (ln (2 + d)).
sym_dilation Bthe symmetric matrix [[0, B], [B^T, 0]] of size m + p of B : 'M[R]_(m, p)

Gaussian matrix series. For symmetric H_1, ..., H_r in 'M[R]_n, S = \sum_j H_j^2 and g of law gauss r, the series X = \sum_j g_j H_j satisfies E tr X^(2q) <= (2q - 1)!! opnorm S^(q-1) tr S for every q >= 1, with no dimension in the bound (an integral in the extended reals). It follows from E tr X^2 = tr S, which needs no symmetry, and the recursion E tr X^(2q+2) <= (2q + 1) opnorm S E tr X^(2q). The recursion comes from Gaussian integration by parts in each coordinate, E tr X^(k+1) = \sum_j E tr (H_j D_j) with D_j the derivative of X^k along H_j. Lemma 4.2 of the fourfold ergodic paper of family 154 of the OpenAI math collection (revision 1581d4cbd).

The moment bound E tr X^(2q) <= (2q - 1)!! opnorm S^(q-1) tr S, as an integral of a nonnegative function in the extended reals.

Main result, as the README states it

Gaussian matrix series. For symmetric H_1, ..., H_r in 'M[R]_n, S = \sum_j H_j^2 and g of law gauss r, the series X = \sum_j g_j H_j satisfies E tr X^(2q) <= (2q - 1)!! opnorm S^(q-1) tr S for every q >= 1, with no dimension in the bound (an integral in the extended reals). It follows from E tr X^2 = tr S, which needs no symmetry, and the recursion E tr X^(2q+2) <= (2q + 1) opnorm S E tr X^(2q). The recursion comes from Gaussian integration by parts in each coordinate, E tr X^(k+1) = \sum_j E tr (H_j D_j) with D_j the derivative of X^k along H_j. Lemma 4.2 of the fourfold ergodic paper of family 154 of the OpenAI math collection (revision 1581d4cbd).
