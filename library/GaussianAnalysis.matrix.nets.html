A finite eps-net of a set S of column vectors is a finite sequence N such that every point of S lies within Euclidean distance eps of a point of N. The operator norm of a matrix is controlled by its values on a net of the unit ball or of the unit sphere (opnorm_net, opnorm_net_bilinear, opnorm_net_sphere, opnorm_net_sym). Every set in the ball of radius r of 'cV[R]_n has an eps-net of at most (2 e (1 + 2 r / eps))^n of its own points (net_exists, unit_ball_net, unit_sphere_net). The proof rounds each point to a grid of mesh h = eps / (2 sqrt n) and counts the grid points: a point of the ball has at most 2^n C(n + k, n) possible roundings, with k = floor(2 n r / eps).

Main result, as the README states it

If N is an eps-net of the unit ball, then opnorm A <= max_{y in N} |A y| / (1 - eps), opnorm A <= max_{u, v in N} |u^T A v| / (1 - 2 eps), and for symmetric A, opnorm A <= max_{v in N} |v^T A v| / (1 - 2 eps). The bilinear bound also holds for nets of the unit sphere.

Main result, as the README states it

Every set in the ball of radius r of 'cV[R]_n has an eps-net of at most (2 e (1 + 2 r / eps))^n of its own points; for the unit ball and the unit sphere, r = 1. Proved by rounding to a grid of mesh eps / (2 sqrt n) and counting the grid points.
