Lower bounds on probabilities from the first moments of a real random variable X on a probability space P, and the facts on square integrable variables they need.

  • paley_zygmund: for X square integrable and 0 <= theta < E X, P(theta <= X) >= (E X - theta)^2 / E X^2, without sign condition on X.
  • paley_zygmund_sqr: the same applied to X^2, a small-ball estimate from the second and fourth moments: for X in L^4 with E X^2 > 0 and 0 <= t < 1, P(t E X^2 <= X^2) >= (1 - t)^2 (E X^2)^2 / E X^4.
  • expectation_sqr_le: (E X)^2 <= E X^2 for X square integrable.
  • integrable_sqr_Lfun2, Lfun2_sqr, Lfun4_sqr: X is in L^2 when it is measurable and X^2 is integrable; X^2 is in L^1 when X is in L^2, and in L^2 when X is in L^4.
