Let W = goe_mx a, with a of law gauss (n * n). Lemma 5.13 of Koehler and Sohn (arXiv 2608.27372, lines 5820-5860) averages the diagonal of U D U^T over a Haar matrix U, and MathComp-Analysis has no Haar measure on the orthogonal group. Item G6 of phase 5 proves what the paper uses the lemma for, that the diagonal of F(W) is nearly constant for an equivariant F, from the invariance of the law of W under orthogonal conjugation, with Householder reflections in place of the Haar matrix. Every result holds for every n.

  • goe_conj_integral: the law of W is invariant under W |-> Q^T W Q for every orthogonal Q. The map a |-> mxvec (Q^T (vec_mx a) Q) is linear with an orthogonal matrix conj_mx Q (conj_mx_orthogonal: it preserves the Frobenius product, mxdot_conj), Q^T goe_mx a Q = goe_mx (a conj_mx Q) (goe_mx_conj), and gauss (n * n) is invariant under orthogonal maps (integral_gauss_mulmx).
  • goe_equiv_form: for F equivariant (conj_equivariant) and every unit row vector u, u F(W) u^T has the law of F(W)_00, because the Householder reflection that maps the first basis vector to u^T (householder_swap) conjugates W into a matrix of the same law. Hence each diagonal entry of F(W) has the law of F(W)_00 (goe_equiv_diag), and so has (F(W)_ii + F(W)_jj) / 2 + s F(W)_ij for i != j and s = 1 or -1 when F(W) is symmetric (goe_equiv_pair, with u = (e_i + s e_j) / sqrt 2).
  • goe_diag_var_identity: if moreover F(W) is symmetric and E F(W)_ij^2 is finite, then E[F(W)_ii F(W)_jj] + 2 E[F(W)_ij^2] = E[F(W)_00^2] for i != j (goe_equiv_pair2), hence E[diag_var F(W)] = 2 / (n + 2) E[|F(W)|_F^2 / n - (tr F(W) / n)^2]. This is the identity of the proof of Lemma 5.13 (lines 5853-5860), where the rho_i are the eigenvalues of F(W).
  • goe_diag_var_tail: for C >= 0 and eps > 0, P(diag_var F(W) >= eps) <= 2 C / ((n + 2) eps) + P(|F(W)|_F^2 > C n), by the identity for the map W |-> F(W) when |F(W)|_F^2 <= C n and 0 otherwise, which is still equivariant, and Markov's inequality (gauss_markov). So diag_var F(W) tends to 0 in probability when |F(W)|_F^2 / n is bounded in probability, as for F(W) the positive part (psd_part of matrix/psd_part.v) of W - w I with w bounded, which is how Proposition 5.15 uses Lemma 5.13.

Lemma 5.13 of Koehler and Sohn without the Haar measure. For F equivariant with F(W) symmetric and E F(W)_ij^2 finite, E[diag_var F(W)] = 2 / (n + 2) E[|F(W)|_F^2 / n - (tr F(W) / n)^2], where diag_var A is the empirical variance of the diagonal of A. Without moment assumptions, P(diag_var F(W) >= eps) <= 2 C / ((n + 2) eps) + P(|F(W)|_F^2 > C n) for every C >= 0 and eps > 0.

Main result, as the README states it

Lemma 5.13 of Koehler and Sohn without the Haar measure. For F equivariant with F(W) symmetric and E F(W)_ij^2 finite, E[diag_var F(W)] = 2 / (n + 2) E[|F(W)|_F^2 / n - (tr F(W) / n)^2], where diag_var A is the empirical variance of the diagonal of A. Without moment assumptions, P(diag_var F(W) >= eps) <= 2 C / ((n + 2) eps) + P(|F(W)|_F^2 > C n) for every C >= 0 and eps > 0.
