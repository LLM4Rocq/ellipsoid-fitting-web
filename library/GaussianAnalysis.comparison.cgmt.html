Theorem 5.3 of Koehler and Sohn (arXiv 2608.27372, lines 4972-4990; Thrampoulidis, Oymak and Hassibi, Theorem 3), in a self-contained form that suffices for the paper's limits. Let A be an N x M matrix of independent standard normal entries (A = vec_mx a, a of law gauss (N * M)) and g, h independent with laws gauss N and gauss M (g = lsubmx z and h = rsubmx z, z of law gauss (N + M)). For a nonempty set K of 'cV[R]_M with |x| <= rho on K, let

Phi(K) = inf_(x in K) |A x|                  (escape_inf K A, escape.v),
phi(K) = inf_(x in K) (|g| |x| - <h, x>)_+   (cgmt_aux K g h).

Then:

  • cgmt_lower: for N > 0, E Phi(K) >= E phi(K) - rho;
  • cgmt_upper: for K closed and convex, E Phi(K) <= E phi(K) + rho;
  • cgmt_escape_tail and cgmt_aux_tail: Phi(K) and phi(K) are at least u away from their means with probability at most 2 exp (- u^2 / (2 rho^2)) and 2 exp (- u^2 / (4 rho^2)) (gauss_concentration.v);
  • cgmt_tail: for N > 0 and K closed and convex, Phi(K) is at least rho + u away from E phi(K) with probability at most 2 exp (- u^2 / (2 rho^2)).

In the paper's application rho is O(d) while Phi and phi are normalized by d^(3/2), so the error rho vanishes: the normalized Phi and phi have the same limit in probability, which is what the comparisons (eq:cgmt-comparisons) of the paper are used for.

  • cgmt_aux_mean_zero, cgmt_zero_tail: if phi(K) vanishes with probability more than 1/2, then E phi(K) <= 2 rho sqrt (ln 4), and Phi(K) exceeds rho (1 + 2 sqrt (ln 4)) + u with probability at most 2 exp (- u^2 / (2 rho^2)): the approximate solutions that the CGMT gives when the auxiliary problem has the value 0.
  • measurable_escape_inf, measurable_cgmt_aux: Phi(K) and phi(K) are measurable functions of the Gaussian entries (they are Lipschitz), so that their laws are defined.
  • For a set K of row vectors, the theorems apply to the set tr_set K of their transposes: escape_inf_tr_set and cgmt_aux_tr_set write Phi and phi as infima over K, and tr_set K is nonempty, bounded, closed or convex with K (tr_set_neq0, tr_set_bounded, closed_tr_set, convex_tr_set).

The proofs. gordon_gamma is Gordon's inequality (gordon.v) for finite families rs u and cs t: the min-max over u and t of |rs u| <g, cs t> + |cs t| <h, rs u> is in mean at most that of the bilinear process plus gamma |rs u| |cs t|, gamma an independent standard normal. infsup_compare extends it to bounded index sets by eps-nets (nets.v). The lower bound takes the index sets K and the unit ball, where sup_(|y| <= 1) (|x| <g, y> + |y| <h, x>) = (|g| |x| + <h, x>)_+; the term in gamma costs at most E |gamma| rho <= rho, and the sign of h changes by the symmetry of gauss. The upper bound exchanges the index sets and uses the minimax exchange escape_inf_minimax: for K closed and convex, inf_(x in K) |A x| <= sup_(|y| <= 1) inf_(x in K) <y, A x>, by the variational inequality at a minimizer of |A x| on the compact set K (closed_bounded_compact_cV).
