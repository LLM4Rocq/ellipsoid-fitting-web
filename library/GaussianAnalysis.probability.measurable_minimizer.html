Let g t, for t in a measurable space T, be functions on a set D of row vectors, minimized at th t with quadratic growth:

g t (th t) + c |theta - th t|^2 <= g t theta  for theta in D, c > 0.

If the values g t (q m) at the points of a fixed sequence q of D are measurable in t and come arbitrarily close to the minimum, then t |-> th t is measurable: th t is the limit of the first minimizers of g t among q 0, ..., q k. This gives a measurable choice of the minimizer of the least squares objective with a barrier of minimizers.v as a function of the features; it does not depend on the features that the objective does not use, as for the leave-one-out objectives of Koehler and Sohn (arXiv 2608.27372, proof of Proposition 2.1, lines 4525-4595).

first_min g q kthe first minimizer of g among q 0, ..., q k, with its value
ls_reduced Z P s lambda beta gamma B thetathe objective with a barrier at (theta, ls_bopt theta), the best b for theta
ls_min DV scB B0 D0 Z P s lambda beta gammathe minimizer over D of ls_reduced

Main results:

  • measurable_minimizer: the selection lemma above.
  • rel_open_dense_seq: a nonempty relatively open convex set D has a sequence of points of D that is dense in D.
  • ls_minP, ls_min_growth: (ls_min Z, ls_bopt (ls_min Z)) minimizes the objective with a barrier on D x R, and the reduced objective at theta in D exceeds its minimum by at least beta lambda |theta - ls_min Z|^2.
  • measurable_ls_min: Z |-> ls_min Z is measurable.
  • ls_min_tuple_set: ls_min does not change when a feature outside P does.
