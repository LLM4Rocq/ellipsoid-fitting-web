Uniform means on finite types

For a finite type T and f : T -> R, unif_mean f = #|T|^-1 \sum_x f x is the mean of f under the uniform law of T, and unif_var f = unif_mean (f - unif_mean f)^2 its variance; R is a realFieldType. A tuple of m independent uniform coordinates is a uniform element of m.-tuple T, so these means express the expectations of functions of independent uniform random elements of finite sets as finite averages, without measure theory.

  • Linearity, monotonicity and constants (unif_meanD, unif_meanZ, unif_mean_sum, ler_unif_mean, unif_mean_cst), extensionality (eq_unif_mean, eq_unif_var), invariance under bijections (unif_mean_bij, unif_var_bij), and the mean of the indicator of a predicate as the proportion of the elements that satisfy it (unif_mean_indicator).
  • Fubini: the mean over T1 * T2 is the iterated mean (unif_mean_pair, unif_mean_comm); the mean over m.+1.-tuple T is the mean over the head of the mean over the tail (unif_mean_tuple_cons), and the mean of a product over the coordinates is the product of the means (unif_mean_tuple_prod). The uniform law of T1 + T2 is the mixture of the uniform laws of T1 and T2 with weights #|T1| and #|T2| (unif_mean_sum_type).
  • The variance: Var f = E f^2 - (E f)^2 (unif_varE), Var f <= E (f - c)^2 for every c (unif_var_le), (E f)^2 <= E f^2 (unif_mean_sqr_le), and the law of total variance for a pair and for the head of a tuple (unif_var_pair, unif_var_tuple_cons).
