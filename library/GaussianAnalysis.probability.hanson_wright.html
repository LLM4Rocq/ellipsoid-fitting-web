Let x have law tuple_prod mu n, so that X_i = f_i (x_i) are independent, centered and subgaussian with constant K (subgaussian.v), and let A be a real n x n matrix. The quadratic form Q = \sum_(i, j) a_ij X_i X_j (hw_quad A) concentrates around hw_center A = \sum_i a_ii E X_i^2, which is its mean (an identity this file does not need): the inequality of Hanson and Wright in the form of Rudelson and Vershynin (Vershynin, High-Dimensional Probability, theorem 6.2.1), with explicit constants. This is item HW of phase 5 (Lemma 3.2 of Koehler and Sohn, arXiv 2608.27372, lines 2157-2185).

  • hanson_wright: for u >= 0, P(|Q - E Q| >= u) <= 2 exp (- min (u^2 / (6912 K^4 |A|_F^2), u / (136 K^2 opnorm A))), with 6912 written 4 * 1728 in the statement; hanson_wright_upper is the one-sided bound.
  • hanson_wright_subexponential: Q - E Q is subexponential with every constant L >= 252 K^2 |A|_F.
  • hanson_wright_mgf: E exp (l (Q - E Q)) <= exp (1728 l^2 K^4 |A|_F^2) when 68 |l| K^2 opnorm A <= 1. Q - E Q is the diagonal part \sum_i a_ii (X_i^2 - E X_i^2) (hw_diag_mgf, Bernstein's inequality, subexponential_sum_mgf) plus the off-diagonal part (hw_quad_split), and exp of their average is at most the average of the exps.
  • hw_offdiag_mgf: E exp (l \sum_(i != j) a_ij X_i X_j) <= exp (432 l^2 K^4 |A|_F^2) when 1152 l^2 K^4 opnorm A ^ 2 <= 1. The off-diagonal sum is 4 / 2^n times the sum over the sets S of the decoupled sums \sum_(i in S, j notin S) (offdiag_decouple, since a quarter of the sets contain i and not j: sum_set_indicator, by the involutions set_toggle), and expR_avg_le is Jensen's inequality for exp and a finite average.
  • hw_decoupled_mgf: E exp (l \sum_(i in S, j notin S) a_ij X_i X_j) <= exp (27 l^2 K^4 |A|_F^2) when 72 l^2 K^4 opnorm A ^ 2 <= 1. Resample the coordinates outside S (ge0_integral_tuple_prod_merge of conditioning.v), apply Hoeffding's inequality to them, write the result as a Gaussian integral (integral_gauss_expR_dot: E exp <g, w> = exp (|w|^2 / 2)), exchange the integrals (fubini_tonelli), apply Hoeffding's inequality to the coordinates in S, and bound the Gaussian chaos for the matrix hw_mask S A, whose norms are at most those of A.
  • gauss_chaos_mgf: for M : 'M_(n, m), 0 <= t and 4 t opnorm M ^ 2 <= 1, E exp (t |g M|^2) <= exp (3 / 2 t |M|_F^2): g M has the law of h B with B = diag (sqrt d) P for the spectral decomposition M^T M = P^T diag d P (ge0_integral_gauss_mulmx), and |h B|^2 is a weighted chi-square.
  • subexponential_of_mgf: if E exp (l Y) <= exp (l^2 s^2) for |l| b <= 1, then Y is subexponential with every constant L >= 3 max (2 s, b).

The Hanson-Wright inequality: for x of law tuple_prod mu n with X_i = f_i (x_i) centered and subgaussian with constant K, and A a real n x n matrix, Q = \sum_(i, j) a_ij X_i X_j satisfies P(|Q - E Q| >= u) <= 2 exp (- min (u^2 / (6912 K^4 |A|_F^2), u / (136 K^2 opnorm A))), and Q - E Q is subexponential with constant 252 K^2 |A|_F; E Q is written \sum_i a_ii E X_i^2 (hw_center). Proved by decoupling over the sets S of coordinates, Hoeffding's inequality twice and a Gaussian chaos bound, and Bernstein's inequality for the diagonal.

Main result, as the README states it

The Hanson-Wright inequality: for x of law tuple_prod mu n with X_i = f_i (x_i) centered and subgaussian with constant K, and A a real n x n matrix, Q = \sum_(i, j) a_ij X_i X_j satisfies P(|Q - E Q| >= u) <= 2 exp (- min (u^2 / (6912 K^4 |A|_F^2), u / (136 K^2 opnorm A))), and Q - E Q is subexponential with constant 252 K^2 |A|_F; E Q is written \sum_i a_ii E X_i^2 (hw_center). Proved by decoupling over the sets S of coordinates, Hoeffding's inequality twice and a Gaussian chaos bound, and Bernstein's inequality for the diagonal.
