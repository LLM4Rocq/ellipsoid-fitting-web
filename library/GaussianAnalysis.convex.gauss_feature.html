  • extension/Features/Regular.v

(2.10) of Koehler and Sohn (arXiv 2608.27372): the centered Gaussian law with covariance S^T S, the law of x S for x of law gauss k, is a (3^(1/4), sqrt 2 opnorm (S^T S))-regular feature (regular_feature of least_squares.v) when S != 0. The moments needed are those of the quadratic forms of a standard Gaussian vector.

  • gauss_quad_moments: for A symmetric and x of law gauss k, E x A x^T = tr A and E (x A x^T)^2 = (tr A)^2 + 2 |A|_F^2, by the spectral theorem and the invariance of gauss k under orthogonal maps.
  • gauss_mxdot_moments: E <x, a> = 0, E <x, a> <x, b> = <a, b> and E <x, a>^4 = 3 |a|^4.
  • gauss_regular_feature: (2.10), for a law nu given by its values, nu A = gauss k [set x | A (x S)].

Theorem gauss_regular_feature k (S : 'M[R]_(k, N))
    (nu : probability 'rV[R]_N R) :
  (forall A, measurable A -> nu A = gauss k ((fun x => x *m S) @^-1` A)) ->
