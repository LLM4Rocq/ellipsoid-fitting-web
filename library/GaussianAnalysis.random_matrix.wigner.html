Let W = goe_mx a for a of law gauss (d * d) (random_matrix/goe.v). The empirical law of its eigenvalues spec W (matrix/von_neumann.v) converges in probability to the semicircle law of radius 2. This is the form of the semicircle law that Lemma 5.14 of Koehler and Sohn (arXiv 2608.27372, line 5905) cites from Anderson, Guionnet and Zeitouni. This file completes item G7 of phase 5.

Proved:

  • weierstrass_lipschitz: Weierstrass's approximation theorem on a compact interval for Lipschitz functions, by Bernstein polynomials (not in MathComp-Analysis 1.16): bernstein_lipschitz, |B_n f (x) - f x| <= L / (2 sqrt n) on [0, 1] for f L-Lipschitz, from the mean and the variance of the binomial law (bernstein_mean, bernstein_var).
  • segner: Segner's recurrence c_(n+1) = \sum_(i <= n) c_i c_(n-i) for a sequence with c_0 = 1 and (i + 2) c_(i+1) = (4 i + 2) c_i, by sums symmetric under i |-> n - i (the Catalan numbers, without generating functions).
  • semicircle_catalan: the moments m_k of semicircle_prob 2 satisfy the recursion of the Schwinger-Dyson equation of goe.v in the limit, m_(k+1) = \sum_(r < k) m_r m_(k-1-r), from segner and the moment recursion and the odd moments of semicircle_law.v.
  • goe_moment_cvg: for W = goe_mx a with a of law gauss (d * d), E tr W^k / d tends to the k-th moment of semicircle_prob 2 as d tends to infinity, by induction on k with goe_tr_sd of goe.v, whose covariance terms tend to 0 (goe_tr_cov_cvg, from goe_tr_var_small and Rintegral_cov_le); the same holds for the eigenvalues clipped to [-3, 3] (goe_clip_cvg).
  • wigner: Wigner's semicircle law. For f continuous and L-Lipschitz on [-3, 3], and eps > 0, P(|goe_stat f a - \int f dsigma| >= eps) <= eps for every large d, where goe_stat f a = (1 / d) \sum_i f (spec W 0 i) and sigma = semicircle_prob 2. Off the event opnorm W >= 3, whose probability tends to 0 (goe_opnorm_tail_small), the statistic is that of f o clip 3, which is Lipschitz (clip_comp_lipschitz); its mean tends to \int f dsigma by polynomial approximation on [-3, 3] (goe_stat_clip_mean_cvg, with goe_stat_poly_clip, semicircle_poly and semicircle_clip), and it concentrates around its mean (goe_stat_conc: goe_stat g is sqrt 2 L / d-Lipschitz in a, goe_stat_lipschitz).
  • wigner_uniform: the same uniformly over a family f_t, t in [t0, t1], of functions L-Lipschitz on [-3, 3] and L-Lipschitz in t on [-3, 3]: for every large d, the event that |goe_stat f_t a - \int f_t dsigma| >= eps for some t lies in a measurable event of probability at most eps. A grid of step eps / (3 (|L| + 1)) in t, wigner at each grid point, the union bound (Boole_inequality), and the event opnorm W >= 3 again; between grid points goe_stat_near and semicircle_near. Lemma 5.14 uses it for the functions (x - t)_+ and (x - t)_+^2.
  • wigner_uniform_inf: under the same hypotheses, with t0 <= t1, the infimum over t in [t0, t1] of goe_stat (f t) a converges in probability to the infimum over t of \int f_t dsigma: for every large d, the samples where the two differ by at least eps lie in a measurable event of probability at most eps. It follows from wigner_uniform at eps / 3, with inf_adherent; no minimum needs to be attained.
